# The New Workout Mat That Guides Your Exercise Form

[The New Workout Mat](https://reboundworks.escortskhi.com/)

[New Workout Mat That](https://benpepsi.com/blog/2105190555.html)

Exercises that require you to step into space can be tricky—dangerous, even— when it comes to body positioning and proper form. Take the lateral lunge, for example. When you step out to the side, does you foot always land in the same spot? And are you stepping out as far as you did the last time you did this exercise?

There’s more at stake here than just proper form. Without some kind of way to measure your movement, you might be increasing your flexibility from week to week without even realizing it, robbing yourself of the opportunity to do the exercise with a wider range of motion or even move on to something more challenging.

In the yoga realm, TrüFORMAT has done a great job of creating a functional (and guy-friendly) mat with guiding form markers hidden in its stylish scorpion-like design.
