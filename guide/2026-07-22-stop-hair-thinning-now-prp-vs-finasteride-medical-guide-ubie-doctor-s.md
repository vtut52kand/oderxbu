What Is PRP Therapy?

Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) uses your own blood components to stimulate hair follicle activity.

Process

A small blood draw.

Centrifuge separates platelets and growth factors.

Injection into thinning scalp areas.

What Is Finasteride?

Finasteride is an oral prescription medication that blocks the enzyme 5-alpha reductase, reducing DHT levels.

MechanismPrevents conversion of testosterone to DHT, the hormone linked to follicle miniaturization.

DosingTypically 1 mg once daily. Best taken at the same time each day.

Studies show about 65% of men maintain or regrow hair after one year.

Deciding Which Treatment Is Right for You

Consider these factors when choosing between PRP and Finasteride:

Severity & Pattern of Loss

Early, mild thinning: both options may help.

Advanced bald spots: PRP alone may be less effective; consider combination therapies.

Budget & Insurance

Finasteride is generally lower cost with insurance.

PRP is often out-of-pocket and varies widely.

Tolerance for Procedures

PRP involves injections every month.

Finasteride is a simple pill with minimal clinic visits.

Risk Tolerance

PRP carries virtually no systemic risk.

Final Thoughts

Hair thinning can have multiple causes and no one-size-fits-all solution. PRP and Finasteride are both medically approved, backed by credible research, and represent two distinct strategies:

PRP harnesses your body's natural growth factors.

Finasteride targets the hormonal driver of genetic hair loss.

Choosing between them depends on your goals, budget, risk tolerance and lifestyle.
