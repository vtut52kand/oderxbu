If you are enrolled in Medicare or any Medicare-related insurance plans(e.g., Medicare Advantage), you agree that neither you, Midi, IWC, nor any health care organization or provider we partner with will submit claims for reimbursement to Medicare or any Medicare-related insurance plans for services or products provided by Midi.
