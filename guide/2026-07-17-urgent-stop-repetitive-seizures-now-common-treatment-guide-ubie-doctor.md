Internal Medicine

Repetitive seizures are typically treated with medications called benzodiazepines. These fast-acting drugs can be administered in several ways, including orally (by mouth), intranasally (through the nose), rectally, or by injection, depending on the situation and severity. Common benzodiazepines used include diazepam, lorazepam, and midazolam. In some cases, longer-term anti-seizure medications may also be prescribed to help prevent future episodes.

If you or a loved one are experiencing repetitive seizures or unexplained symptoms, understanding the potential cause is the critical first step toward effective treatment.
