Andrea Culotta, MSN, APRN, FNP-C

Guided by connection: "I’ve built my career in reproductive endocrinology and women’s health, caring for women through infertility, postpartum recovery, and now the midlife transition. I love helping women navigate these meaningful stages, making sure they feel supported, informed, and at their healthiest through each one."

Moments that leave an impact: "Some of the most rewarding visits are when a patient says I explained something about her health that no one had ever taken the time to before. Education is empowerment, and I want every woman I see to leave feeling more confident and in control of her care."

Collaborative care: "I believe in shared decision-making. Especially in midlife, there’s no one-size-fits-all approach. My role is to educate, listen, and guide patients toward evidence-based decisions that feel right for them, so we move forward as partners with a shared goal of helping them feel their best."

A movement in motion: "I’m inspired to be part of Midi because we’re revolutionizing women’s midlife care. It’s exciting to work alongside clinicians who are just as passionate about creating the care women deserve."
