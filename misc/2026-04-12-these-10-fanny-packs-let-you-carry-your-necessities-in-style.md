The fanny pack—also called a waist pack, sling bag, or crossbody bag—provides a great amount of cargo space in a compact design, which holds your essentials like phone, keys, wallet, etc. These styles can be strapped to your body, and are more secure than a tote bag, and less bulky than a backpack. That’s what makes fanny packs ideal for everyday carry, travel, working out, and outdoor adventures.

What to Consider

Shopping for a fanny pack may seem straightforward, but there are a few key specs to keep in mind before selecting one. Here's a rundown of what to consider.

Volume and Capacity

First up, think about what you want to carry. Most fanny packs provide an interior capacity in liters, usually running somewhere between 1 and 5 liters. Small packs (1-3) are ideal for small essentials; think your phone, wallet, keys or lip balm. Medium packs (3-5) can handle additional items such as sunscreens, post-workout snacks, and small cameras. Large packs (5+ liters) are for those who need to carry a bit more, like tablets, large water bottles, or even a packable jacket. In terms of physical size—check the dimensions and depth to ensure it fits your body comfortably and isn’t too bulky.

Material and Durability

Nylon and Polyester

These two fabrics are lightweight, durable and often water-resistant, which makes them great for active use, travel, and general day-to-day wear.

Canvas

This type of material is sturdy enough to take a beating. It has a more rugged charm and vintage style that looks good for outdoor activities like camping and hiking.

Leather

If you want a super stylish, timeless fanny pack, opt for one made from leather. It adds a classy touch to any outfit and has a longer lifespan than other materials.

Pockets and Organization

Pockets are essential on fanny packs because they help keep your belongings organized and easily accessible. Usually, the main compartment is the largest space for your primary items like your phone, wallet, keys, etc. Then there’s specialized pockets, like zippered or hidden ones, that add extra security for your valuables. Some pockets are made to carry water bottles to keep you hydrated as you’re on the move. All in all, the quick access saves you from having to rummage around to find what you need. Pockets also help distribute the weight evenly, making the pack more comfortable to wear.

Weather Resistance

Typically, the best fanny packs for men are treated with a DWR (durable water-repellent) coating that repels light rain and splashes. Look out for fully sealed seams and zippers to keep water out completely.

How to Style Fanny Packs

There are two schools of thought when it comes to styling fanny packs. The more traditional approach is to treat it like any other bag. You wear your favorite backpack or tote over pretty much any outfit because it is a utilitarian accessory, just there to carry your stuff from point A to point B. If that is your purpose for wearing a fanny pack, then invest in a versatile color like black, blue, or gray. It won’t clash with anything, and adds a little extra twist to your look while you’re on the go.

The more contemporary use of a fanny pack is as an outfit accessory, like a belt or hat. In this case, you will be wearing it most of the day, and it will be a fully integrated part of your outfit. This is the intention of most luxury brands that are making fanny packs, which is why you’re seeing sleeker designs and expensive materials like leather and suede. Wearing your fanny pack across your body is the preferred look in this case. As with any accessory, you want to blend colors and patterns. That's why black is overwhelmingly the most popular color for desginer fanny packs, because black goes with everything.
