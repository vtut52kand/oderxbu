Who Should Avoid CJC-1295 with DAC?

While CJC-1295 with DAC is generally well-tolerated when used responsibly but certain individuals should approach it with caution:

Individuals with Cancer or a History of Cancer: CJC-1295 stimulates the release of growth hormone (GH) and IGF-1, which are associated with cell growth and regeneration. Anyone with a current or past diagnosis of cancer should avoid CJC 1295 with DAC unless recommended by a medical professional.

People with Diabetes or Insulin Sensitivity Issues: Since IGF-1 has insulin-like effects and can alter glucose metabolism, individuals with type 1 or type 2 diabetes or those with insulin resistance may experience blood sugar fluctuations. Individuals with Cardiovascular Conditions: Elevated levels of GH and IGF-1 may affect blood pressure, water retention, and heart rate. People with hypertension, heart disease, or stroke history should consult a healthcare provider before use.
