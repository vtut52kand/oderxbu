Internal Medicine

Cinnamon may support weight loss by improving how your body processes sugar and fat. Studies suggest it can help regulate blood sugar, reduce insulin resistance, and curb cravings—key factors in managing weight. Simple ways to add cinnamon to your diet include sprinkling it on oatmeal, blending it into smoothies, stirring it into coffee, or mixing it into yogurt.

However, unexplained weight changes, cravings, or blood sugar issues can sometimes signal an underlying health concern that cinnamon alone won't fix.
