What Are Dad Shoes?

Dad shoes generally refer to practical, comfortable sneakers popularized by fathers or middle-aged men back in the day. Performance-wise, dad shoes are often running shoes or workout shoes with oversized, chunky soles that provide maximum comfort for guaranteed underfoot support and cushioning. Design-wise, dad shoes are known for their bold color combinations that do not concern with fashion trends since dad shoes should put comfort first, style second.

What to Look for in Dad Shoes?

With an emphasis on all-day comfort, today’s best dad shoes need to feature plush cushioning in the footed or midsole to provide cloud-like support for your feet during walking or standing. However, in addition to just walking shoes, the best dad shoes will also be versatile enough for training, (trail) running, hiking, or just going about every day. The best dad shoes should also showcase the classic style, with a chunky profile and a balance of neutral tones and pops of color that ensures versatility for styling, allowing you to effortlessly wear them with various outfits.

How to Choose Dad Shoes

Cushioning and support: The best dad shoes should prioritize comfort by having ample cushioning in the midsole, footbed, and heel area. This will provide support and impact absorbing during wear and walking to reduce fatigue. In addition, since most dad shoes are running or athletic shoes, they should also have arch support for proper foot alignment and minimizing discomfort, especially if you'll be wearing the shoes for extended periods.

Design: The hallmark of dad shoes is the chunky, oversized sole. But the sole should still offer flexibility and responsiveness for walking and running. In addition, dad shoes should not be minimalist in design: there should be some sort of color-blocking or wild colorways.

Material: Dad shoes are often made in high-quality materials such as leather, suede, or mesh that contribute to the longevity of dad shoes, and help the footwear withstand extended periods of wearing, walking, and running.

Activity: What you do in dad shoes matters, since dad shoes are often athletic shoes that cater to a variety of activities. The classic, iconic dad shoes are often running shoes that can also be worn for walking long distances, but many are also retro sneakers for cross-training or working out. In general, more often than not, dad shoes are great for everyday wear and walking.

How to style with dad shoes?While they appear to be built for the sidelines of mini-league games, dad shoes are surprisingly suitable for many occasions. Fortunately, today’s styles have been updated to offer a laid-back style as well as comfort. But perhaps the real motivation to wear dad shoes today is because they’re so damn comfortable.
