# A New 'Bill & Ted' Movie Is Totally Happening

[Movie Is](https://jointrevive.xianfengchem.com/)

Truly excellent news has arrived: A third Bill & Ted movie is finally happening! Keanu Reeves and Alex Winter, who played the titular characters in Bill & Ted’s Excellent Adventure and its supremely excellent sequel Bill & Ted’s Bogus Journey, made the announcement in a video today.

The threequel, entitled Bill & Ted Face the Music, will start production this year and is currently scheduled to be released in summer 2020. Set 30 years after their first adventure, this film will revisit former teen rockers Bill S. Preston, Esq. and Theodore “Ted” Logan in adulthood.

“Yet to fulfill their rock ’n’ roll destiny, the now middle-aged best friends set out on a new adventure, when a visitor from the future warns them that only their song can save life as we know it and bring harmony to the universe,” states the film’s official synopsis. “Along the way, they will be helped by their families, old friends, and a few music legends.”

Bill and Ted became cult comedy characters in 1989’s Excellent Adventure, which saw the pair travel through time, meeting historical figures such as Napoleon Bonaparte, Billy the Kid, and Greek philosopher Socrates. In the 1991 sequel, Bill & Ted’s Bogus Journey, the two best friends ended up in the afterlife and crossed paths with none other than the Grim Reaper, in a famous sequence that paid loving, irreverent homage to Ingmar Bergman’s The Seventh Seal.
