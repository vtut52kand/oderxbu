Our product picks are editor-tested, expert-approved. We may earn a commission through links on our site. Why Trust Us?

YOUR WORKOUT ROUTINE can—and should—change during the summer months. Once the weather starts to shift for the better, you might feel the pull of the great outdoors. Heed that call. Whether you're at the park, beach, or your own backyard, one of the best ways to stay in shape in the summer is to get outside and get moving.

The one downside of outdoor workouts: Unless you have a world class home setup filled with award-winning gear, you no longer have access to a full gym's worth of weights, machines, and gear. That's not to say that you don't have options, however. Bodyweight workouts are a great option, and you can use the environment around you—a playground bar or sturdy tree branch can serve as a reliable pull up station, for example—but you can also get your own equipment that works well with the DIY workout ethos you have to embrace to get a good session outside the gym.
