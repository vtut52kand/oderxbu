Wegovy harnesses this mechanism by lending a stronger and more prolonged version of this hormonal messaging. You May Have Hit a Weight Loss Plateau

Even with well-designed lifestyle changes and supportive medication, plateaus are nearly inevitable in weight-loss journeys. After initial progress, your body may learn to adapt to the lower calorie intake, slowing metabolism as a protective mechanism. This is often referred to as “metabolic adaptation,” where your body effectively becomes more efficient and burns fewer calories during rest and activity 8. While frustrating, it does not need to be permanent.

To break through a plateau, you can slightly adjust your exercise routine, try new forms of physical activity, or fine-tune your diet. Sometimes increasing resistance training, which helps build muscle mass, can give your metabolism a boost. Or you could try cycling your caloric intake by eating a bit more on some days and slightly less on others to keep your body from settling into an energy-conserving rut.
