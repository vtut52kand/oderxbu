What is Roseola?

A viral infection causing fever and rashes on the trunk and neck. It may also be accompanied by irritability, mild diarrhea, decreased appetite and swollen eyelids. It is most common between 6 and 15 months of age.

Typical Symptoms of Roseola

Skin over the entire body is red

Rash that started after the fever disappeared

Fever

Bumpy rash that appeared, then rapidly spread to the rest of the body

Skin problem on the chest, tummy or back

Fever but feeling relatively well

Redness in the affected area

Swelling around the front and sides of the neck

Diagnostic Questions for Roseola

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Is your whole body red?

Did you have a few days of high fever, then a rash when the fever went away?

Do you have a fever?

Did a small skin bump spread all over your body?

Are there any skin problems on your stomach, chest, or back?

Treatment of Roseola

The infection typically goes away on its own. Treatment focuses on easing symptoms, such as using painkillers, creams, and staying hydrated. In some instances, doctors may suggest antiviral medications for kids with weak immune systems.
