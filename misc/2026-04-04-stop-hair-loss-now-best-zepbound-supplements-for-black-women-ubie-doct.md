Doctor's Note: The Best Hair Loss Supplements for Black Women Taking Zepbound

Hair loss can be a concern for anyone, but Black women may face unique challenges due to hair texture, styling practices, and underlying health factors. If you're taking Zepbound (tirzepatide)—a medication used to help control blood sugar and support weight loss—you might notice changes in your hair's thickness, growth rate, or fullness. While definitive research on Zepbound and hair loss is still emerging, several evidence-based supplements and lifestyle strategies can support hair health without creating undue worry.

Understanding Zepbound and Hair Health

Zepbound (the brand name for tirzepatide) is a dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonist. Its main roles include:

Improving blood glucose control in type 2 diabetes

Promoting weight loss by curbing appetite

Potential effects on hair:

Rapid weight loss can sometimes trigger temporary hair shedding (telogen effluvium).

Changes in nutrient absorption or appetite may lead to micronutrient gaps critical for hair growth.

The good news? Hair loss linked to these factors is often reversible with proper nutrition, gentle hair care, and time.

Biotin (Vitamin B7)

Role: Supports keratin production, the main protein in hair.

Typical dose: 2,500–5,000 mcg daily.

Note: Higher doses aren't always better; start low and monitor.

Vitamin D

Role: Regulates hair follicle cycling and may prevent premature follicle dormancy.

Typical dose: 1,000–2,000 IU daily (or as directed by blood testing).

Note: Low vitamin D levels are common and associated with hair thinning.

Iron

Role: Carries oxygen to hair follicles; deficiency can lead to diffuse thinning.

Typical dose: 18 mg daily (or based on lab results).

Note: Always check serum ferritin—too much iron can be harmful.

Zinc

Role: Modulates scalp oil production and DNA/RNA synthesis in hair follicles.

Typical dose: 8–11 mg daily.

Note: Excess zinc can interfere with copper absorption; don't exceed 40 mg/day.

Omega-3 Fatty Acids

Role: Anti-inflammatory properties that support scalp health.

Sources: Fish oil (EPA/DHA), flaxseed oil.

Typical dose: 1,000 mg combined EPA/DHA daily.

Collagen Peptides

Role: Supplies amino acids for keratin; supports overall tissue repair.

Typical dose: 5–10 g daily.

Protein Intake

Role: Hair is ~90% protein.

Aim: At least 0.8 g protein per kg body weight; higher if active.

Lifestyle and Hair Care Tips

Supplements work best when paired with gentle hair care and scalp health:

• Protective Styling

Low-manipulation styles (twists, braids) reduce breakage.

Avoid excessive tension and ultra-tight edges styles.

• Moisture Retention

Use water-based leave-in conditioners and seal with oils (e.g., jojoba, castor).

Deep-condition weekly to maintain elasticity.
