# Elliot Page Shows Off His Cut Physique in a Shirtless Selfie

Elliot Page is looking lean as hell in his latest Instagram post. The Umbrella Academy star uploaded a shirtless thirst trap on Sunday, and his defined six-pack abs are on full display.

"Oh good my new phone works," Page wrote in the caption for the post, which accrued nearly half a million likes within half an hour of being posted. (It's the kind of perfectly unnecessary, disingenuous half-justification for posting a gratuitous pic that has become an integral component in the art of the celebrity thirst trap. Just ask Luke Evans.)

"You’re gonna break that phone tho with your intense hotness," wrote Ian Daniel, Page's co-host on the VICE docu-series Gaycation. The photo also provoked messages of thirst and approval from several of Page's other famous friends, including 13 Reasons Why's Tommy Dorfman, Shang-Chi star Awkwafina, Emily in Paris actress Ashley Park, artist and activist Rain Phoenix, actress Dani Vega, and comedian Megan Stalter.

It's not the first time Page has posted thirst on main. This summer, he celebrated getting his first pair of swimming trunks with a photo, which also happened to be his first shirtless picture since undergoing top surgery. He has since kept track of his progress in sculpting those abs on his Instagram Story.
