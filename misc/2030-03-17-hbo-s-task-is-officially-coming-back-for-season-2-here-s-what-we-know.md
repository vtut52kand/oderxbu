And for a show about some pretty grisly criminal behavior, Task has an ending that's ultimately pretty optimistic—our heroes all do the right thing, the bad guys get what they deserve, and, at the end of the day, the sun is shining brightly in the Outside Philly area. Despite the hardships that the world and happenstance sometimes force people (and some of our favorite characters) into, Inglesby seems to argue, good people want to do good things for other good people. And despite the evil very present in the world, and the bad choices many bad people choose to make, that goodness can still sometimes win out.

Task ends with a neat little bow, and a satisfying end to our story. But this is television, and with successful television there's always going to be a desire for more. Given that Tom is still alive and well—and he's still played by the always fantastic Ruffalo—it seems natural to wonder whether there could be another case for him to pursue, another task force for him to lead, and another season of Task for us to watch.

Will Task get a season 2?

Yes! HBO officially announced that Task will return for a second season on November 20, 2025.

"Rarely does a writer balance humanistic storytelling with intricate, explosive plotting but Brad Ingelsby is one of our industry’s greats and we have no doubt he will strike as profoundly and addictively once again in season two," HBO's head of Drama Series and Films Francesca Orsi said in a press release.

What will Task season 2 be about?

We know for sure that Mark Ruffalo will return for the second season of Task as Tom Brandis, but we don't know much else beyond that. Given that Inglesby has had such success telling his stories in that 'Outside Philly' area, we must assume he will keep his new story set there as well.

As far as story beyond that? It's hard to speculate too much, although it's clear that watching Ruffalo's Tom get to the bottom of a case while simultaneously battling his own issues and demons is just a straight-up good time. He will, however, need a couple good foils to fill the shoes left vacant by the likes of Pelphrey and Jamie McShane along with supporting players like Alison Oliver and Fabien Frankel.We're also just going to have to throw out the idea of a potential Mare of Easttown and Task crossover. Does it seem likely? No, probably not. Would it be super fun to see Ruffalo's Tom and Kate Winslet's Mare cross paths at some random Wawa? Yes, it certainly would.
