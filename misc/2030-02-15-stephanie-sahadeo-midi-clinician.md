Stephanie Sahadeo, MSN, APRN, WHNP-BC

Helping women through every stage: “I’ve been a Women’s Health Nurse Practitioner for nearly a decade, starting in reproductive endocrinology and infertility before moving into complex gynecology. I’ve treated everything from abnormal bleeding to perimenopause and menopause, as well as surgical prep and recovery. No matter the stage of life, my focus has always been on listening, validating, and providing real solutions for women’s health concerns.”

Because I’ve been there too: “As a minority woman, I’ve personally experienced the bias and dismissal so many of my patients describe. I know what it’s like to feel unheard, to be told something is 'nothing' when you know it isn’t. That’s why I listen… really listen. If something is wrong, we’ll figure it out together. If it can’t be changed, we’ll talk about the best ways to manage it.”

Care as unique as you: “When you visit me, expect a conversation, probably with some humor thrown in. Talking about menopause should feel as comfortable as chatting with your girlfriends, because we should be talking about it! Every woman’s journey is different, and we work with you to find real answers, real solutions, and a path forward that fits you.”
