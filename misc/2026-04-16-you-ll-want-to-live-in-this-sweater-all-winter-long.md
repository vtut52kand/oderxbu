After a long day of skiing, nothing feels better than a hot shower and a hot drink at the lodge bar. Make sure you’re armed with a warm, comfortable post-mountain outfit that looks just as great as it feels—starting with this soft alpaca sweater. The Fair Isle print gives it a cool après ski vibe (re: after skiing activities) without looking sloppy, and the nylon-wool stretch pants wear like a tailored version of sweats. The dark color combination elongates your frame, too—ideal if you’re still working off that holiday weight gain. Pair the base with thick mid-calf socks to keep your feet warm, and functional winter boots to keep them dry. We like this Frye pair, which has cool tweed detailing to break up the stark black. The shearling lining feels great on wiped-out feet (Learn how to style your pants with boots here.). For your final layer, a light gray vest is unexpected, but still on par with your post-run look. Toss it on over the sweater, and leave it open so the Fair Isle pattern is visible. Finish the look with cool matte sunglasses and a warm trapper hat. This outfit also works off the slopes. Skip the accessories and swap the pants for dark denim for a weekend dinner outfit. Instead of the vest, opt for a warmer winter coat before you head outside.
