# Building a Real Death Star Would Cost About 18 Quadrillion Dollars

[Death Star](https://pepenails.com/ex0o24h/)

Now that Space Force and relaunchable rockets are just things we take for granted, it may seem like only a matter of time before we graduate to X-Wings, Imperial Cruisers, and maybe—just maybe—our very own Death Star. In a recent episode of the YouTube and Facebook Watch series What If, which explores what it would take for various fantastical and apocalyptic scenarios to play out in real life, the show hosts try to imagine the resources it would require for a moon-sized superweapon to come into being.

In short, don't count on it happening anytime soon: The actual size of the Death Star, according to their estimations, is close to 120 to 160 kilometers in diameter, and would cost the equivalent of 18 quadrillion dollars to build. (If you're curious how they've sourced all of this information, the show's creators have conveniently provided some backup.)

Creating and transporting the steel required, they also note, could take as long as 860,000 years and would likely leave the planet so polluted that everyone on Earth would have no choice but to fight for a spot onboard the spacecraft. Even then, you shouldn't necessarily feel too secure: The recoil created by a planet-vaporizing blast would send the Death Star flying into oblivion at a rate of 77 kilometers per second. (Unless, of course, the ship had an anti-matter device also onboard.)

The show creators do recognize at least one silver lining, however: Instead of destroying planets, a Death Star could be used for a far more benevolent purpose: vaporizing any potentially planet-crushing asteroids that enter the solar system, provided we can find a way to foot the $8 octillion bill required to fire the thing. Now we just need our newly-recognized Space Force to accelerate that 860,000-year timeline.
