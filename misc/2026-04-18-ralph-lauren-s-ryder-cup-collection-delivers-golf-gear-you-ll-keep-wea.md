# Ralph Lauren’s Ryder Cup Collection Delivers Golf Gear You’ll Keep Wearing All Fall

TEAM USA LOST the Ryder Cup, but they definitely out-dressed the competition. That's thanks to Ralph Lauren, which outfitted the squad—including Captain Keegan Bradley—in patriotic golf shirts and technical jackets that look great on and off the links.

The latest Ryder Cup collection comes from Ralph Lauren’s RLX line, its under-the-radar golf imprint. First launched in 1998, RLX has grown to infuse Polo’s all-American vibe with performance fabrics that are built to handle wind, sweat, and rain. Translation: Team USA’s red, white, and, blue fits were as sharp as they were tough.

SHOP THE RALPH LAUREN RYDER CUP COLLECTION

You don't have to be a pro to rock this gear. Ralph Lauren's Ryder Cup collection can be shopped on the brand's website, and it goes way beyond just golf polos. There's a satin map jacket with NYC embroidery that plays as well downtown as it does at the country club, a rugby sweatshirt that slots right into your weekend wardrobe, and a hybrid jacket built for unpredictable fall weather. It's the latest run of Ralph Lauren sports collabs—from the U.S. Open to Wimbledon—that proves the All-American giant still knows how to turn event gear into timeless sportswear.

At Men's Health, we've covered how younger menswear brands are cashing in on golf's newfound cool factor, especially with Gen Z. Lululemon and Adidas are dropping hyper-technical polos and pants, while buzzy upstarts like Malbon are infusing their link fits with streetwear. But our style editors agree: Ralph Lauren still owns the space. Seven Ryder Cups later, the All-American giant is showing once again why its mix of heritage and performance tech is the look every other brand wants to copy.
