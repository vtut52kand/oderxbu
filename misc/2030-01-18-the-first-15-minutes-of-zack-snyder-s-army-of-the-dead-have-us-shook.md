# The First 15 Minutes of Zack Snyder's 'Army of the Dead' Have Us Shook

[Zack Snyder's Army](https://calmvector.dns0663.com/blog/6911858433.html)

Get your pick-axes, machetes, and other sharpened weapons ready—Zack Snyder's Army of the Dead is nearly here. Even better, Netflix just unleashed the first 15 minutes of the movie on YouTube.

In the clip, we witness what appears to be patient zero for the zombie infection and the messy aftermath of his (its?) escape to Las Vegas. Flesh-hungry showgirls chow down on a Vegas performer. A zombified Elvis impersonator roams the streets. Dave Bautista kicks ass, as per usual. And in the video's final moments, we witness the destruction of sin city, with nothing but zombies left in the ruins of the town. Frankly, it's one of the best opening sequences to a zombie flick we've seen in years.
