# The Best Way to Build Your Biceps

Your biceps are relatively small as muscles go, but that doesn't stop you from cranking out rep after rep of biceps curls in hopes of bigger guns. "However, the classic dumbbell curl only targets the biceps brachii, one of the two muscle groups in the front of your upper arm," says Todd Durkin, C.S.C.S., author of The IMPACT! Body Plan. To sculpt sleeve-busting muscle, you need to focus on the brachialis—the muscle that lies under the biceps brachii—too, he says.

So what's the best way to do that? Just switch your grip during a dumbbell curl, says Durkin. In this weekend's fitness challenge, you'll use a hammer grip (palms facing each other) which forces your brachialis to work harder the entire movement. Building this muscle can boost strength and make your arms look thicker.

[Build Your Biceps](https://peplawllc.com/blog/2675091138.html)

Here's how to do it: Hold a pair of 35-pound dumbbells at arm’s length next to your sides, with your palms facing each other. Without moving your upper arms, curl the dumbbells upward, bringing them as close to your shoulders as you can. Pause, and then lower the weights back to the starting position. Perform as many hammer curls as possible in 2 minutes. You can stop whenever you need to, but keep the clock running the entire time. Ready to try it?
