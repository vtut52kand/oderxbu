What is MRSA Infection?

Methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus (MRSA) infection is caused by a type of bacteria that has become resistant to many antibiotics used to treat common infections. It is divided into two groups: community-associated or healthcare-associated MRSA infection and is common in people with weak immune systems.
