# BBQ Rules & Fat Loss Nutrition for Men

I get just as many nutrition questions as I do fat loss questions, so I always end up interviewing Dr. Chris Mohr, Ph.D. He's the industry's go to guy for weight loss nutrition.

Not only has he worked with hundreds of individuals to enhance their weight loss, but he too had a weight problem at a young age, so lives the lifestyle himself to make sure he doesn’t go back down that path. Here's the latest interview I did with Chris.

Anecdotally what we’ve seen is that men tend to follow everything we tell them to a ‘t’ without veering off that path; they follow the exercise guidelines, nutrition guidelines, and the behavioral principles to make sure they lose fat.

Women, on the other hand, seem to question our strategies, put everyone else first and don’t make time for themselves, and let their emotions rule the process a little more. Men just do as their told…usually.

And of course hormones can play a role in not only fat loss, but where that particular fat is stored. There are data to show that testosterone preferentially shuttles more fat to the abdominal area, while estrogen is more protective against this midsection weight gain (known as visceral fat).

We can see this difference when looking at men and women; men tend to have more midsection weight gain, while women tend to carry more weight in their hips and, err, the backside. As we age, testosterone levels naturally decrease in men and estrogen levels decrease in women; during this transition and changing of hormones, we see changes in where folks store their fat.

It does seem as if visceral fat (again, think midsection weight gain) is easier to mobilize than what’s known as subcutaneous adipose tissue (e.g., “cellulite”). And it seems the best type of exercise for decreasing midsection weight gain is higher intensity exercise, like what you promote in your fat loss workout.

Of course this has to be coupled with a sound nutrition plan and an increase of overall physical activity each day---meaning do your high intensity physical activity, move more throughout the day, and consume an overall calorie reduced, but high nutrient diet.

Learn the Secrets to Grocery Shopping for Fat Loss

If you tailgate 7 days a week, your risk of successfully losing fat will come to a screeching halt, but if you enjoy it once in awhile, you’re good to go. When you’re BBQ, here are a few rules to remember:

1. If you’re hosting, make sure you have grilled chicken, pork, or fish on hand. Something like kabobs would be great—colorful veggies, lean chicken breast (or even pork would be a great option) and you’re sacrificing good taste at all, just getting the best of both worlds.

2. Want some juicy red meat? Be sure to have 100% grass fed lean beef available (grass fed because of the beneficial fatty acid profile I’ll discuss in a bit).

3. Limit the heavy, mayonnaise based salads, such as potato or macaroni salad, and instead try some fresh veggies, side salad, fresh corn on the cob, tomato salad, and things along those lines. Always emphasize the color of the diet—the more color you can eat, the better off you’ll be. 4. Limit alcohol—enjoy in moderation, but be sure to alternate calorie free, non-alcoholic drinks more often than not.5. Get your exercise is first thing in the morning because your chance to do so as the day goes on will be less and less.
