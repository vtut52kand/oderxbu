What are the common side effects of hormonal birth control pills?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Hormonal birth control pills commonly cause side effects such as nausea, headaches, breast tenderness, and mood changes. Less common effects may include spotting between periods, weight fluctuations, and decreased libido. Most side effects are mild and tend to improve within 2–3 months as your body adjusts to the hormones.
