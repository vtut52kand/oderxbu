3) Bulgur wheat.

As your whole grain intake goes up, your overall cancer risk may drop, a review in BMJ suggests. It could be the fiber, which helps keep you full by slowing down how fast sugar is released into your bloodstream, suggests Carrie Daniel-MacDougall, Ph.D., M.P.H., researcher at MD Anderson Cancer Center. Bulgur has over 50 percent more of it than quinoa does.

TIP: Swap your morning oatmeal for bulgur; toss it into salads at lunch; or prepare it with garlic, scallions, and ginger and top with trout or salmon for an easy dinner.
