Dr. Pimple Popper's latest video is going to have the cross section of people who love Pixar and cyst removals very happy. The dermatologist, whose real name is Dr. Sandra Lee, is tasked with removing a massive cyst from a patient's head. As she's attempting to pull the round-shaped growth free, it's hard not to see its resemblance to a famous Pixar character.

The moment the video begins, viewers will understand why Dr. Lee chose to add the hashtag #mikewazowski to this Instagram video. She has made an incision over the bulbous cyst which makes it resemble an eyeball according to one comment. For fans of Pixar's Monster's Inc, though, the growth now looks just like Mike Wazowski's signature feature — his eye.

But this is still a Dr. Pimple Popper video, so no matter how badly you may want this Wazowski-looking cyst to stick around to scare some children, Dr. Lee has to remove it. Steadily, the dermatologist begins to apply pressure and push the skin back which causes the cyst to roll around in the patient's head before it emerges bit by bumpy bit. Eventually, she's able to get the growth exposed enough to snip it free of her patient's skin, and sadly, any resemblance to Mike Wazowski is gone.
