WE DON'T KNOW about you, but as soon as that telltale autumn snap enters the air, all we want to do is get cozy. Of course, that means busting out our sweaters and jackets, but it also means switching up our cologne. If you’ve never thought about changing up your scent according to the seasons, we urge you to give it a shot. Just like most things, such as food, clothes or outdoor activities, cologne is an ideal seasonal switch. Plus, much like seasonal produce, the ingredients in a fragrance can evoke the vibes of a specific season effortlessly and effectively.

Think of the notes you often find in fragrance and they sound like a grocery list for the ultimate fall vibes: spices, incense, smoke, woods, leather—sometimes even foods like vanilla, nuts and fruit. Fall colognes may or may not include all of those things, but what they all have in common is that they tend to be warmer, deeper and cozier than the kind you typically wear in warmer weather.

It's not a hard and fast rule, but because of the nature of the notes, they typically last a little longer, too. So when in doubt, if you're wondering whether something is a fall cologne, think about the notes, but also consider the overall vibe. If it makes you feel warm, then you’re on the right track. No matter what kind of cologne you like, there’s a fall version out there for you and these are our favorites.

Fragrance Family

Fragrances are typically grouped into families, five to be exact: Woody, Floral, Fresh, Amber and Spicy. There are other subcategories (like gourmand, fougere, and chypre), but generally, all fragrances can fit into one or more of these overarching families. You’ll find fall-appropriate colognes in every family, but if you want to know the overall vibe of a fragrance, knowing what family it belongs to is a great first step.

Notes

The notes of a fragrance are kind of like the ingredients. It’s the specific mix of these notes that give the cologne its unique scent. Knowing what notes you gravitate toward can help you know if a fragrance may have a scent you like (for instance, if your favorite cologne contains sandalwood, you may like other sandalwood fragrances), but also keep in mind that many notes will smell different depending on what they’re mixed with and the amount of that note in the recipe.

Size

Size doesn’t factor into how a cologne smells, but it can factor into your purchase. Fragrances can often be expensive, but they can also last a long time depending on how much you wear them, so buying a larger size if it’s something you wear often, could help drive down the cost per wear.

Dior Sauvage Eau Forte

We wouldn’t necessarily consider the original Sauvage a fall cologne, though it is a solid bet for all seasons (it’s the best-selling men’s fragrance in the world, after all), but the new Eau Forte isn’t the original. Not only does it have a slightly different ratio from the classic Sauvage notes, but it’s also water-based (instead of alcohol-based), which allows the notes, especially the spices, to smell smoother and warmer.

Think of this as painting with watercolors whereas the original version is like painting with spray paint. There is still a freshness to this version, but it’s not sharp or citrusy; instead, it’s more subtle and blends with the spices to bring them out instead of competing with them. We think it’s a perfect choice for daytime wear during the fall—crisp like the autumn air, but warm and sexy like you’re wearing a cashmere sweater.

Tom Ford Oud Wood

If we’re talking fall colognes, you really can’t go wrong with woods. Woody notes come in all forms, but the warm, smokey quality they often provide is an ideal scent for all. Think of a forest as the leaves turn or sitting around a fire or chopping wood—what could be more autumnal than that? And if we’re talking about woody colognes, we can’t omit Oud Wood.

At this point, the original Oud Wood has entered the pantheon of greatest men’s colognes of all time, but this new version takes it to a whole new level. The scent is very similar, but this one, since it’s a parfum, is more concentrated making it bolder and longer lasting. This blend of spices and woods results in a smell that's both sophisticated and masculine. While we prefer it for the evening, it’s wearable during the day, too, which makes it a great option for a signature fall scent.

Stetson Legend

Like all Stetson colognes, this one is inspired by the American West, which doesn’t necessarily seem like an obvious inspiration for a fall fragrance but think about notes like leather, smoke, mesquite wood, cedar, sandalwood and sage and tell us those don’t scream fall vibes.
