# Burn Fat and Build Muscle With This Push-Pull Routine

When it comes to lifting weights, opposites can really attract. We’re huge fans of push-pull workout routines—that is, exercises that hit both the “push” (presses) and “pull” (rows) functions of your body, working out two different muscle groups at once.

[Muscle With This Push-Pull](https://incretin-path.escortskhi.com/)

Dan Flores, CEO at Fitlink, recently stopped by to show us his push-pull routine, as you’ll see in the video above.

“Today I’m going to show you four moves, four rounds, twenty reps,” he says. “Choose a moderate weight if you want to build muscle.”

Then he lays it out: high row, bent-over row, overhead press, and push-up.

[This Push-Pull Routine](https://skinsequence.escortskhi.com/blog/5515529858.html)

For the high rows, you’ll need a cable machine. Sit on the floor and pull the weight toward you, sitting upright and tall, and squeeze when it comes to the bottom.

[Burn Fat and](https://cortexcompass.jcesqc8.com/blog/0260329098.html)

For the bent-over row, you’ll need a pair of dumbbells. Keep your back flat, and squeeze at the top. Both row variations hit different parts of your back for a more complete upper body back workout.

The push portion of the routine starts with overhead presses, getting a good burn in the shoulders. “Take it straight overhead, follow through to the top,” says Flores. Cap the push portion of the workout off with traditional push-ups, working your chest and triceps.

Do each move for five reps, so you’ve got 100 total reps in the workout. We love awesome push-pull upper body routines, and we can’t wait to add this one to our arsenal.
