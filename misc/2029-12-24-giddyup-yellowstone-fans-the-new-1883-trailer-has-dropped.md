# Giddyup, ‘Yellowstone’ Fans, the New ‘1883’ Trailer Has Dropped

If you’re a Yellowstone fan, good news is coming your way. Yes, the main series is finally back for Season 4, but it looks like we’ll be seeing much more of the Dutton family; A prequel to the popular series is currently in the works, and coming way sooner than you probably expect.

Created and produced by Taylor Sheridan, a co-creator of Yellowstone and writer of movies like Hell or High Water, Wind River, and Sicario, the prequel, titled 1883, will explore where the Dutton family got its start. Since the already-popular Yellowstone hit all-time highs with its Season 3 in 2020 (and Season 4 seems to be following suit), we expect quite a bit of excitement for what’s in store.

Now with one month left before the December 19 release date, we have a trailer to keep us pumped for what's to come. In case you thought it wouldn't be as good as the original series, the teaser alone shows 1883 is probably going to be another hit for Paramount.

Here’s everything we know so far about 1883.

What will 1883 be about?

A teaser for the new series aired during Super Bowl LV. The clip featured an unknown voice that says, “I don’t care if you live or die. But should you live… you tell them about me,” leaving fans with no real expectations. But according to a recent statement from Paramount, 1883 will follow the Dutton family’s attempt to flee poverty and seek a better life in Montana, traveling across the Great Plains in a retelling of Western expansion.

There's shots of burning houses, a distraught Faith Hill, and Sam Elliott using his pistol to keep the settlers in check.

Who will be in the show?

[the New Trailer](https://memorycircuits.hbsnzj.com/blog/0756979966.html)

According to Hollywood Reporter, Sam Elliott, Faith Hill, and Tim McGraw are set to star in the spin-off series. And honestly, they couldn't have cast it better.

Elliott will play as Shea Brennan, a cowboy with an unfortunate past who's tasked with leading a group from Texas to Montana. McGraw and Hill will play James and Margaret Dutton, in an obvious connection to the original show's present-day Dutton family.

Naturally, Elliott says he's excited for the role and says the Western genre speaks to "The classic struggles of man against man, man against nature, and man against himself. It’s all there, in 1883, and I’m honored to be a part of it.”

When is 1883's release date?

You won't have to wait too much longer. Deadline reports fans can see the Dutton patriarch and matriarch traverse the West on Dec. 19 on Paramount+. That's just about a month after the original series debuted its fourth season on Paramount Network. Which means we'll get a full winter of saddle-riding, cowboy boot-wearing Dutton drama to keep us warm.

How can I watch 1883?

Although the first three seasons of the original Yellowstone are available to stream through NBCUniversal’s Peacock streaming service (and airs first run on TV's Paramount Network (check your local listings)), 1883 is taking a different route. The series will premiere exclusively on Paramount+, the new streaming service that launched earlier this year . The service is available for subscribers through a $5.99/month plan with limited commercials or $9.99/month plan with no commercials.
