# Watch Competitive Eater Leah Shutkever Set a New Donut-Eating World Record

Everyone loves doughnuts, but could you eat 10 in a single sitting? One woman did in order to set a Guinness World Record, and the YouTube video will have you rethinking heading to Krispy Kreme.

The video is from competitive eater Leah Shutkever whose YouTube channel is full of videos showcasing her consuming abilities. She has eaten 100 pieces of sushi and gyoza, four pounds of wings, and over six pounds of tacos. Now, she's taking on the Guinness World Record for most jam-filled doughnuts eaten in three minutes.

In order to beat the current record, Shutkever explains that she has to follow the exact guidelines set forth by the committee. First, she has to eat more than six doughnuts to break the record, but it's not that simple. The other rules include making sure the doughnuts are commercially available, they're the correct size, and they have the appropriate internal temperature. Perhaps the strangest rule, however, is that Shutkever isn't able to lick her lips while attempting to eat the doughnuts.

Once she's gone through all of the requirements, it's time to eat. Shutkever obliterates the challenge. While there are multiple moments, where you'll likely be worried that those doughnuts are about to come back up, by the half way point, she has already conquered the existing record. At the end of the video, she's downed a whopping 10 doughnuts in three minutes.
