# John Cena Apologized to The Rock With a Jug of Moonshine

DWAYNE JOHNSON, AKA The Rock, might be known as one of the nicest guys in Hollywood, but he is not without beef. The wrestler-turned-actor has been on the outs with his former Fast & Furious co-star Vin Diesel for years, but for a long time he was locked into an even more highly publicized feud with his fellow WWE star John Cena.

Johnson and Cena have since patched things up, but as Cena explains, it was a long hard road to reconciliation. In a recent conversation with Howard Stern, he reflects on how their animosity began, and the part he played in it—beginning with the annoyance he expressed when Johnson first began prioritizing his burgeoning film career.

[Rock With](https://huanle3.com/idyh9zmtr/)

"He was openly saying, 'I love the WWE.' And I'm like, 'man, if you love it, why aren't you here?'" Cena recalled. He acknowledges now that it was a "stupid thing to say," especially given how his own career trajectory has mirrored Johnson's, and his movie commitments have taken him away from wrestling for long stretches of time.

"Now I have to eat a bunch of shit and say 'fuck, I was wrong and I'm sorry,'" he said.

Initially, when Johnson did return to wrestling, the two did not speak outside of the ring, and their resentments brimmed over into their matches together. Cena said "there was a lot of stepping over the line of boundaries of what's permissible and trustworthy in the ring and what's not, I violated his trust again by calling him out on some shit... he did the same to me, so we grew farther and farther apart."

This all continued until a WrestleMania event in Miami, where there was some uncertainty beforehand surrounding the narrative of their next fight—and who would win. It was at this time that Cena says Johnson's professionalism helped the two of them to be more genuinely collaborative, and it became possible for them to start talking openly. And Cena knew, having questioned Johnson's dedication to the wrestling that was engrained in his family, an apology was owed.

"First and foremost I went to his mom and I said I was sorry, and I hope she understood everything I was doing was just for business," he continued. "And his mom is an ace, she's amazing. And then I went to him, and I always traveled with a jug of pure Tennessee moonshine, and I went to him with a mason jar, and said 'this is kind of a tradition we have on the road, now I just wanted to share this tradition with you.'"

Since then, Cena says they have been on good terms, and he is quick to state just how much he admires and respects Johnson. "He is truly in a universe of his own," he says. "Every inch of success he has earned, that word is very important, he has earned it. And now, having to try and tread in some of his wake, I see exactly how difficult all that is, and I'm not even a hundredth of his level."
