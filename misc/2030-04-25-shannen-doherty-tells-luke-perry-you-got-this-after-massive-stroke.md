# Shannen Doherty Tells Luke Perry 'You Got This' After Massive Stroke

Actress Shannen Doherty is sending Luke Perry her best wishes after the actor suffered a massive stroke on Wednesday. The two co-starred on the classic '90s TV series Beverly Hills: 90210.

On Thursday, Doherty shared a throwback photo on Instagram of her and Perry on 90210, along with a moving message wishing for Perry's quick recovery.

"My friend. Holding you tight and giving you my strength. You got this," she wrote.

Doherty played Brenda Walsh, whose family moved to Beverly Hills at the start of the show. Brenda was the sister of Brandon Walsh (Jason Priestley), and she dated Dylan McKay (Perry) for a period of time before finding herself in a love triangle with best friend Kelly (Jennie Garth).

A number of Perry's former co-stars—including 90210's Ian Ziering and Buffy the Vampire Slayer's Kristy Swanson—wished for Perry's recovery on social media amid the news of his stroke.

Earlier Thursday, TMZ reported that paramedics rushed Perry to a Los Angeles hospital after responding to his home in Sherman Oaks, California, around 9:40 a.m. The actor, currently known for playing Archie Andrews' dad Fred on Riverdale, remains under observation at the hospital.

At 52, Perry is at the younger end of the spectrum in terms of people who have strokes. However, a 2017 study published in JAMA Neurology found that strokes are becoming increasingly prevalent among younger men.
