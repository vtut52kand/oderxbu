# How Machine Gun Kelly Trains for His High-Energy Live Performances

Musician and rapper Machine Gun Kelly, a.k.a. Colson Baker, turned 30 this year. And staying in shape is more important to him than ever.

He took Men's Health inside his gym to show us the workout he does that focuses on mobility and strength. And it's something he didn't do years ago.

"We're gonna train a little bit today. For those that are wondering if I even trained, fair enough, because this wasn't the case years ago. But a lot of years of doing a lot of stuff has led me to believe that I've gotta keep on living and I've got to get stronger," he tells Men's Health.

In addition to training for his role in the sci-fi action movie Project Power, on Netflix on August 14, his goal is to keep his body limber for his high-energy, high-impact performances.

"My performance on stage the last 10 years I think is unmatched outside of the greats: Prince, Michael Jackson, Rage Against the Machine, Nirvana. I'm just trying to take it to the next level," says Baker, who's releasing his next album, Tickets to My Downfall, later this year. "When you're climbing 60-foot metal scaffolding, arm strength and leg strength and mental strength matters. So, let's get a training session in."

Warmup

"Before we start we're going to get some stretching in. One of my favorite quotes someone told me is, 'Your age isn't how old you are, but how flexible you are'."

1. Adductor Rockback with T-Spine Rotation3 sets of 30 seconds per side

"Put one knee down with one leg out to the side. Reach tot he sky and reach your chest up. Get that stretch in your groin, so that you can use your groin...it's a very important muscle, man!" says Kelly.

2. Staggered Stance Squat10 reps per side

"Take the toe and line it up with the heel of your other foot. Keep your back straight and go down for a nice squat, come back up. Plop down, take a giant sh*t, and then go ahead and come back up," says Kelly.

3. Pushup with Shoulder Tap10 reps

"You want to make sure your core is tight and that your butt and back aren't moving too much. If you set a cup of ice water on your back, it would stay on there. Nice and poised," says Kelly.

4. Three-Way Hip Flexor Stretch9 reps each "way" per side

I think about how Prince was 45 years old, doing full splits on stage but then having to come off and take all these painkillers just to walk around. A lot of my performance on stage reflects that movement. I'm also 6'4", so to crunch my body up and jump off of 20 foot platforms, it takes its toll on your body," says Kelly. "So that we're not 50 years old taking painkillers just to move, we should open up the hip area a little more."

To do it, put one leg out in front of the other and lean into the stretch. Then move your leg 45 degrees out, and then a 90 degree angle.

"And if you aren't doing the splits in two months after doing that, then you didn't do it right!" jokes Kelly.
