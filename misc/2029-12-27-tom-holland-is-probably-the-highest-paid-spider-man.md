Tom Holland has had a good run of playing Spider-Man. The 25-year-old actor has already starred in three successful solo Spider-Man films and helped integrate the character into the Marvel Cinematic Universe by appearing in three more (Avengers: Infinity War, Avengers: Endgame, and Captain America: Civil War). For a relatively young actor, he's done pretty well.

He got his start doing West End theater, starring in the title role for Billy Elliot: the Musical before moving onto film. His breakout role was Spider-Man's first appearance back in 2016, when he made a brief but memorable appearance in Captain America: Civil War, also cementing his connection with the character's pseudo-father figure, Iron Man. Holland's acting as the neighborhood web-slinger also garnered him a British Academy Film Award for Rising Young Star in 2017.

Outside of the superhero genre, Holland's also starred in a few other notable roles, in The Lost City of Z, as Arvin Russell in Netflix's The Devil All the Time, and the upcoming movie adaptation of the video game series Uncharted.

With the highly anticipated (and critically-acclaimed) release of Spider-Man: No Way Home, Holland's Marvel check is no doubt getting bigger and bigger with each major release. His first standalone film, Spider-Man: Homecoming, earned $880 million according to Forbes.The second film in the current Spider-Man franchise, Far From Home, managed to earn $1 billion at the box office, making it the highest-grossing Spider-Man film to date according to First Post. And if a similar franchise-worthy IP like Uncharted really takes off, he'll be in not one, but two film series with dedicated moviegoing fan bases. And with high-paid costars like Robert Downey Jr. and Scarlett Johansson, it wouldn't be a surprise if Holland's net worth was equally as impressive.
