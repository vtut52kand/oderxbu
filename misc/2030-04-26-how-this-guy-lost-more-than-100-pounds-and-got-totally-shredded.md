After he'd fully recovered from surgery, Sher stuck to the promise he made to himself, focusing first on getting himself off the couch. "The first day I was cleared for exercise, I hauled myself down the street and did a lap. One lap turned into two, and day after day I was trying to beat my times and distance," he says. Sher also began to shy away from social and family outings where he knew the temptation to overeat would be strong. Finally, he turned to the internet for advice, combing through forums and subreddits such as r/fitness and r/bodybuilding for practical tips.

At first, cardio alone—along with some minor diet changes—did wonders. In the first six months of his new program, Sher lost about 50 pounds. Then he started to incorporate weight training and lifting six days a week. For more than two years, Sher stayed consistent, and eventually, he managed to melt off another 60 pounds of fat—110 pounds total. "How do I feel? The confidence took a while to catch up, Sher admits, but he got there. "Overall I just felt like I didn't have to hide myself in public," he says.
