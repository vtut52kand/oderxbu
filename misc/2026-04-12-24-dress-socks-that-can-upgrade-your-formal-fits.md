THERE WAS a time when dress socks were boring, uncomfortable foot covers that you had to slip on for work. But nowadays, some of our favorite sock brands have made great strides in looks and comfort. Nowadays, some of the best dress socks for men are worth wearing all the time.

If you want a 24/7 type of dress sock, the best dress socks for men should be made in fabric blends that are moisture-wicking, breathable, and soft—most of them are either cotton or wool socks. The best dress socks for men need to also incorporate elastic materials that will retain the shape of the socks and help them stay in place without causing discomfort or slipping down your legs.

When it comes to style, the best dress socks for men fall into one of two categories depending on the occasion. For more formal occasions like black tie events, the best dress socks should stick to solid dark colors, especially when your outfit or dress shoes contain bold patterns, to keep your formal fits from looking too busy. For more casual outfits, the best dress socks will come in a splash of texture and patterns such as dots, stripes, and other classic geometric patterns to elevate your look—timeless patterns like paisley and florals are also failsafe choices.

To help you keep your feet—and your whole style—looking sharp, we assembled a list of the best dress socks for men out there that work way beyond formalwear.
