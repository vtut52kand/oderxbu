# These Adidas Beer-and-Puke-Proof Sneakers Are Exactly What You Need This Fall

Beer drinkers, allow us to introduce you to what just might become your favorite new shoes: the Adidas Originals Munchen Oktoberfest Made in Germany sneakers. No, these aren’t your typical brown leather kicks that you can find in any old sporting goods store or on Amazon. If it wasn’t obvious from the name, the brand created these with the Munich-based beer fest in mind that’s happening later this month.

But the most interesting thing about them is that they apparently won’t get ruined when someone has a little too much to drink or spills a cold one on them. The brand says they’re made with “a durable puke and beer repellent upper.” That's right, these sneakers won’t let a little vomit—or that guy who bumps into you and makes you splash beer all over the ground—hold you and your feet back from going about your day and night.

The Oktoberfest-themed footwear also includes tons of other design elements that would make them stand out from the rest of your collection at home. For starters, they look like a more sophisticated pair of sneakers thanks to the darker brown color surrounding the shoe, as well as the pattern bookending the iconic logo on the tan heel “to match your Lederhosen.” Adidas also added the word “Prost” in capital letters to the outside of the shoes that could fool anyone into thinking you customized them, and it designed a red and white checked interior, as well as a simple white sole.

You can buy them right now for about $238 online. And if you don’t want to make the investment because you already have more than enough Adidas sneakers in your closet, maybe your other water-resistant kicks will be able to do just as great of a job the next time you head to a beer festival—or at least happy hour.
