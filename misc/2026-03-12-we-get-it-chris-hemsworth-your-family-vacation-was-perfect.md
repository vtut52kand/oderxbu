Let's face it: As much as we love Chris Pratt, Chris Hemsworth, Chris Pine, and Chris Evans, it can be hard to tell the Hollywood actors apart. Besides having the same first name, they all star in our favorite superhero and sci-fi movies; their fitness games are all on-point; if you blurred your eyes and looked at the four of them, you'd be hard-pressed to tell which Chris was which.

To help you keep track of everyone's favorite Chrises, we're rounding up what each of them got up to during the week. Welcome to This Week in Chrises.

Chris Hemsworth: Had a Better Vacation Than You Ever Will

Chris Hemsworth and his wife/workout buddy Elsa Pataky took their family on a pretty damn perfect-looking vacation in their home country of Australia this week. "Warning: if you don’t like beautiful coastal locations stay away from @australia," he wrote in the first of a series of Instagram posts.

Hemsworth showed off his athletic prowess by catching a wave with his daughter clinging to his back, because apparently he's Thor and he can do anything.

We probably shouldn't be surprised — he does, after all, work out in some pretty extreme conditions.

Chris Pine: Admitted He Never Read A Wrinkle in Time Before Getting Cast

At a press junket for his new film A Wrinkle in Time, Chris Pine was asked by Fox reporter Shawn Edwards: “How familiar were you with the source material?” To which the actor simply replied: “Not at all.”

Edwards followed up by asking if he read the book once he got the part. "I read it. It’s a small book," Pine replied. "It was beautifully wrought, and it was so simple in its storytelling. It was helpful to read that because the film is in many ways a kind of perfect distillation of the thematic elements of the book."

Chris Evans: Look at His 'Stache!

Chris Evans has been focusing his attention on his new Broadway play, Lobby Hero. He plays a police officer — so it's no surprise that he's grown a pretty epic mustache.

We've seen Evans with his Lobby Hero mustache before, but the 'stache got some new attention when the actor was snapped by popular Instagram user Mike Summers.

Where does Evans' mustache rank among these other celebs' facial hair? We'll let you be the judge.

Chris Pratt: Shared His Love for the Ladies in His Life

Chris Pratt took to Instagram to celebrate International Women’s Day on Thursday.

“Happy #internationalwomensday !! Big ol’ shout out to the women who run my life, dress me, groom me, babysit me and keep me sane during these grueling press junkets. I would die without you,” he wrote.

"And don’t worry, I’m not forgetting my favorite lady the T-Rex," he added, throwing in a shameless plug for the new Jurassic World movie, which hits theaters June 22.
