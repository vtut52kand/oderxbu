# Karen Menser, Midi Clinician

[Karen Menser](https://glp-insights.xianfengchem.com/blog/3049444454.html)

[Midi Clinician](https://benpepsi.com/blog/4977519688.html)

[Menser Midi Clinician](https://pepvoice.com/)

Karen Menser, DNP, APRN, FNP-C

Bringing expertise to every phase: “As a Family Nurse Practitioner with more than 13 years in OB/GYN care, I have deep understanding of sexual health and menopause and hold a Doctor of Nursing Practice in women’s contraceptive use. I was immediately drawn to women’s health in graduate school, where I saw the trusting relationship between a particular clinician and her patients. I knew that this was something that women not only wanted, but they also needed at all different times in their lives.”

Making time for you: “Menopause can be both stressful and scary, often marked by unique physical, emotional, and social challenges. But, it’s also a time when I encourage women to feel empowered to focus on self-care, growth, and personal goals. I want to be that clinician that provides a listening ear, compassion, and a patient-centered approach to help you navigate this chapter.”

Carrying the mission forward: “I know that Midi will truly be life changing for women and the future of healthcare. I am so excited to be part of this movement, ensuring that each patient feels supported and informed throughout their health care journey.”
