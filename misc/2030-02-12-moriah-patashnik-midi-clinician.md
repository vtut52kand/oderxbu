Moriah Patashnik, MSN, APRN, WHNP-BC

Your healthcare ally: “I’ve always loved caring for women. I began as a doula, and my passion for sexual and reproductive medicine led me back to school to become a Women’s Health Nurse Practitioner. My favorite part of caring for women is helping them to make informed decisions that align with their unique needs and knowledge of their body.”

Inspired by my mother’s menopause: “My mom, a cardiologist, has been an incredible influence on my care philosophy. Going through menopause, she chose to stay on hormone therapy after carefully reviewing the evidence, during a time when guidance was conflicting. Her example taught me the importance of providing midlife women with the thoughtful, evidence-based support they deserve in an area that’s been underserved for far too long."

Welcoming women to midlife: “I believe every woman deserves to feel seen, heard, and respected. My approach is grounded in empathy, evidence, and collaboration, tailored to each patient’s story. There’s nothing more rewarding than helping women who’ve felt dismissed finally receive the care they need, empowering them to thrive during this transformative stage of life.”
