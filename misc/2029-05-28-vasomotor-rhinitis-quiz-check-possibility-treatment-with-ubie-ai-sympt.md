What is Vasomotor Rhinitis?

A condition where temperature changes cause symptoms like runny nose, blocked nose, and sneezing.

Typical Symptoms of Vasomotor Rhinitis

Nose is affected by changes in weather

Runny nose

Stuffy nose

Nasal voice

Sensitive nose symptoms improves or worsens with different seasons

Presence of clear, colorless nasal discharge

Runny or blocked nose is worse than the cough or throat pain

Sneezing

Diagnostic Questions for Vasomotor Rhinitis

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Does your nose run when exposed to strong smells or temperature changes?

Do you have a runny nose?

Do you have a stuffy nose?

Does your voice sound nasal?

Do you have seasonal issues with your nose, throat, or cough?

Treatment of Vasomotor Rhinitis

Prevent episodes by avoiding triggers, such as sudden temperature changes. Various nasal sprays can also relieve symptoms.
