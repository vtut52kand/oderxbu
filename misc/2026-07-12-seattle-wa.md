Book IV therapy appointment online: Once you’ve decided which IV treatment you want, you can just select the “Book” button at the bottom of the listing. You’ll find steps on the screen to help you complete your online booking.

Get a complete medical screening for our nurse: Everyone receiving mobile IV infusions must undergo a full medical screening by our nurses. Our nurses will advise you about our full catalog of IV therapies and provide professional care.

Review the aftercare instructions: After every IV drip, our registered nurses will explain the aftercare instructions. Your aftercare documentation will have instructions on what to do if an emergency arises.
