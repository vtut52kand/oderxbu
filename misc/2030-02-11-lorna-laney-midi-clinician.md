Lorna Laney, MSN, APRN, FNP-C

A lifelong commitment to women’s health: "After graduating from UCLA with a Master’s in Nursing, I became a nurse practitioner. Since then, I’ve worked in a variety of women’s health and family practice clinical settings across Southern California. I’ve cared for women through many stages of life and have developed a deep understanding of how their health needs evolve over time."

Experience across every stage of care: "Over the years, I’ve learned that meaningful care begins with listening. When patients feel heard, they can have honest conversations and create care plans that truly reflect their lives. This allows us to focus on shared decision-making and take the time to review options so each woman feels informed and confident in her choices."

Compassionate care, decades of experience: "I’m especially passionate about caring for women in midlife and beyond, a time when many are balancing work, family, relationships, and caregiving all at once. My goal is to support you in a way that feels practical, compassionate, and sustainable."

Supporting women through every stage of life: "With Midi, I have the time to educate and empower you, providing education, clarity, and guidance around your healthcare concerns through an evidence-based, individualized approach. We work closely together to build care plans that reflect your preferences and goals. It’s about making healthcare feel supportive, accessible, and patient-centered."
