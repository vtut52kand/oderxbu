Throat swelling is the inflammation or enlargement of tissues in the throat, often felt as a lump, tightness, or fullness. Common symptoms include difficulty swallowing, hoarseness, sore throat, and in some cases, trouble breathing. Causes range from infections and allergies to acid reflux or more serious conditions.

Explanation

Throat swelling can cause various sensations that might feel uncomfortable or alarming. One common feeling is a sensation of a lump in the throat, often referred to as 'globus pharyngis.' This sensation can make it feel like there is something stuck in the throat, even when there is not.

Another sensation is tightness in the throat, which can sometimes come and go. This tightness can make it difficult to swallow or can cause a feeling of pressure around the neck area.

These sensations can be linked to several causes, including conditions like gastroesophageal reflux, where stomach acid irritates the throat, or other medical conditions that might need attention from a healthcare provider.
