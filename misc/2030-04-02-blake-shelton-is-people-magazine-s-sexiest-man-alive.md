# Blake Shelton is 'People' Magazine's Sexiest Man Alive

People Magazine revealed its 2017 Sexiest Man Alive on Wednesday, and the honor goes to country singer and The Voice coach Blake Shelton.

“Thank you People!!!!,” Shelton tweeted about the honor. “Don't hate me because I'm beautiful…”

So what made Shelton worthy of the honor shared by guys like Brad Pitt, George Clooney, and last year’s winner, Dwayne “The Rock” Johnson?

“Blake Shelton is sexy because he’s sweet—the kind of guy who would pull over and fix your flat tire,” People’s editors wrote. “A guy who says he tries, every day, to put the woman he loves on a pedestal. This year’s list of sexy men is full of men like him—men who use their gifts and their talents to make the world a better place.”

Shelton almost didn’t accept the honor, telling People that it was his girlfriend Gwen Stefani who convinced him to take it.

“She goes, ‘Listen to me, you’re going to regret this for the rest of your life if you don’t take this gift and just live in the moment,” Shelton explained.

The singer can also thank Stefani for helping get his 6’5’’ frame in shape. In April, Stefani revealed to Entertainment Tonight that she and Shelton hit the gym together as often as they can.

Shelton shared his workout secrets with People, saying he tries his best to stay in shape by working out on the treadmill and eating healthy meals whenever he can. Shelton admitted he enjoys some indulgent favorites every now and then, especially when he’s home in Tishomingo, Oklahoma.

“It could be 2 o’clock in the afternoon before I eat and then I realize I’m starving, so then it’s a bag of Cheetos,” he said. “And there’s a Sonic in town, they got jalapeno poppers. And then I’m not hungry again until 10 o’clock, well what’s in there? Fishsticks. And like a big pickle, those pickles that come in its own package with juice? It is really bad.”
