Yes, regular exercise and yoga can significantly reduce period cramps. Physical activity boosts circulation, releases endorphins (the body's natural painkillers), and eases muscle tension in the pelvic area. Gentle yoga poses like Child's Pose, Cat-Cow, and Cobra can specifically target menstrual discomfort, while light aerobic exercise improves overall fitness and helps regulate hormones linked to cramp severity.

However, not all period pain is created equal. If your cramps are severe, worsening, or interfering with daily life, they could signal an underlying condition like endometriosis, fibroids, or PCOS—and no amount of yoga will resolve that alone.
