Salmonella infection, often referred to as salmonellosis, can make people feel quite ill. Key symptoms include:

Fever: People often experience a high temperature, which is the body's way of fighting off the infection.

Stomach Pain: This can feel like cramping or discomfort in the belly area.

Diarrhea: Loose or watery stools are common and can happen several times a day.

Fatigue: Feeling very tired or weak is also a frequent symptom.

These symptoms can vary in severity. Some people might have mild symptoms, while others can feel very sick. In some cases, especially if the infection becomes invasive, symptoms can be more serious and may require medical attention. It's important to stay hydrated and rest if you think you have a Salmonella infection.
