# Nick Cannon Shows Off His Gym and Fridge

[His Gym and Fridge](https://collagen-atelier.bbsxyxy.com/blog/9984238118.html)

Nick Cannon—rapper, actor, comedian, TV host—considers himself part of the 5AM club.

[Shows Off His](https://calmvector.dns0663.com/blog/5015205064.html)

So even when his days stretch on with long studio sessions or late nights DJing at the club, Cannon rises and sweats. Of course, having a gym attached to your office and music studio that no one else is allowed to use—even other artists—makes that morning workout easier to squeeze in. “I never really leave this place,” Cannon admitted. And looking at him, we're inclined to believe him.

[His Gym](https://synaptide-lab.snaketongueofficial.com/)

Welcome to the second entry of Gym & Fridge, a new Men's Health video series where we tour celebrities' most intimate spaces. But, of course, these visits aren't just walk-throughs—and once we got to Cannon's crib, the Masked Singer star (holding a giant bowl of oatmeal) quickly threw on a weighted vests and got to work. “I’m one of the only cats who works out and eats the entire time,” Cannon said, wolfing down breakfast beside his overhead press. Somehow, he didn't get cramps.

[Shows Off](https://synaptide-lab.snaketongueofficial.com/)

He moved quickly onto pull ups, handstands on top of cinder blocks, and grabbed some chains to pull a car across his parking lot. Show off.

While the space may be Cannon's gym, he doesn't work out there exclusively—nor does he work out alone. Cannon and his crew often challenge each other to spontaneous street sessions. One example: Someone yells "fire drill!" and the team has to drop and bust out push-ups, no matter where they are.

[Cannon Shows Off His](https://focus-and-calm.sunsimiaosd.com/)

Cannon then showed us his studio, where things got a little weird. Before we knew it, Cannon was playing gospel music, talking turkey meatballs, and waxing philosophic about his ideal choice of water. And that fridge—Nick, what kind of beer is that, exactly?
