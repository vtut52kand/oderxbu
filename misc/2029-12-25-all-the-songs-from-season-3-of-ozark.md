# All the Songs From Season 3 of 'Ozark'

Every season Ozark definitely takes fans on a wild ride, and the third season of the dark crime series definitely didn't disappoint. Season three centered on the family's casino business, and even though it proves to be successful, Marty and Wendy Byrde start fighting for control. And while fan favorites like Helen Pierce and Rachel Garrison are back, it was a new character—Wendy's little brother—that made the most waves this season.

Part of the magic of the series is the music, and the soundtrack for the third season was full of great, haunting tunes. Phil McGowan has been the show's score mixer since season one, Gabe Hilfer is the music supervisor, and Danny Bensi and Saunder Jurriaans are the composers of the series. The music from the show also landed Bensi and Juriaans an Emmy nomination for Outstanding Music Composition for a Series.

And as the music site Bearded Gentlemen Music explains, "Ozark is a show that isn’t afraid to intersperse its action with moments of silence, giving not only its characters but also the viewer time to reflect on the previous scene’s content...Ozark goes a step further though and cleverly places perfectly fitting songs among these scenes. The beauty of this is that the majority of them fit so well that you don’t even notice, you just accept that they pretty much belong there."

Instead of Shazam-ing the songs as you watch, we decided to compile a handy list of the season's tunes—here are the songs featured in season three of Ozark.
