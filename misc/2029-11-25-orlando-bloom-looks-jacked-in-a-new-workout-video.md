# Orlando Bloom Looks Jacked in a New Workout Video

[Orlando Bloom Looks](https://musclemend.sunsimiaosd.com/)

Orlando Bloom is not averse to the occasional shirtless selfie, and over the last year he has shared several clips and photos captured while working out or doing DIY which show off his lean, cut physique. In his most recent post, a trio of short training videos shot during an upper body session and uploaded to Instagram, Bloom is looking swoler than usual, showing off his strength and muscular physique.

In the first video, Bloom can be seen performing a set of barbell bicep curls using an EZ bar, with his jacked biceps and defined chest on full display. The second clip shows him training his core with an ab wheel, and the third and final video shows Bloom performing a rear cable fly, which targets the delts.

"I broke my back when I was 19 and they said I’d never walk again," the 45-year-old Carnival Row star wrote in the caption, referring to just one of many serious injuries he has overcome in his life; he has sustained a broken arm and leg while playing rugby and skiing respectively, and fractured several ribs while performing stunts as Legolas in The Lord of the Rings.

It's likely that Bloom has been packing on the extra muscle for his role in the upcoming crime action drama Red Right Hand, in which he plays a former gangster who tries to start a new life but ends up crossing paths with a dangerous crime boss. Last month, he shared several behind the scenes photos from the set of that movie, including some fake chest and neck tattoos he has been sporting while playing the role.
