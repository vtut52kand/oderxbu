When most people had to bunker down at home due to the onset of the global pandemic, they turned to Netflix for comfort, finding solace in anything to distract from the chaos of the daily news cycle. One of the shows that took off in a major way was a strange, unexpected documentary series called Tiger King, about tiger sanctuary owners and their bitter feud. It culminated in one owner, Joe Exotic, paying for the other owner, Carole Baskin, to be murdered.

Now, after more than a year of fame (and, in most cases, infamy) for its numerous subjects, Netflix decided to revisit its hit and bring it back for a sequel series, Tiger King 2. Not only will the new show cover what Joe’s been up to since the original docuseries, but it’ll delve into Carole Baskin’s own questionable past, which involved her husband’s mysterious disappearance. As the original series showed, many people believe Baskin actually fed her husband to her tigers. Yeah, really.

In the time since the show dropped on Netflix, there’s actually been a fair amount of developments in both Baskin and Exotic’s lives. And in case you wanted even more content based on the show, there’s a detailed podcast and an upcoming dramatic miniseries starring SNL's Kate McKinnon. Oh, and Joe Exotic has a tell-all memoir hitting bookstores later this month.

Exotic was charged with two counts related to the murder-for-hire scheme, along with charges for violating the federal wildlife laws. He’s currently in prison, serving out his 22-year sentence. While in prison, he's written a tell-all memoir due for release in late November. Currently, his lawyer is fighting to get Exotic re-sentenced.

Exotic also says he's been diagnosed with cancer. Tweeting on November 4, Exotic announced he had a biopsy and has an “aggressive cancer” in his prostate. In his handwritten letter posted to Twitter, he said he didn’t want pity, but for the world to come together and help him get released.

“They have the proof I did not do this and there is no reason for the U.S. attorney to drag this out,” Exotic writes. He asked to be released to seek treatment on his own and spend time with his loved ones.

As for his animal park, the Greater Wynnewood Exotic Animal Park closed down back in August 2020, according to BBC News. In May of the same year, a judge awarded Carole Baskin ownership of the zoo. Jeff Lowe ran the zoo in Exotic’s absence before the property was turned over to Baskin.

Presumably, Exotic’s life in prison, and Baskin’s life since the documentary aired will be covered in Tiger King 2, which will premiere on November 17 on Netflix.
