# Seriously, Dude, Don't Get Josh Brolin's 'Deadpool 2' Haircut

[Dude Don't Get Josh](https://mendstride.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/0760622307.html)

[Don't Get Josh](https://collagen-atelier.bbsxyxy.com/blog/2673280401.html)

When Josh Brolin recently showed off his new jacked body for his role in Deadpool 2, we showed you how to follow his workout routine. Brolin reposted the first picture of him as Cable on Instagram, and fans went wild. And while he definitely makes us want to hit the gym, there's something we just can't get behind about his look.

Contrary to what others are telling you, you just shouldn't get Josh Brolin's Deadpool 2 haircut.

Related Video:

It's an undercut, which features close-to-the-skin sides and back with long hair on top. The style had a moment a few years ago, when celebrities like Brad Pitt and Macklemore and every other dude you know sported the hairstyle. Recently, however, the style has somewhat fallen out of favor due to alt-right men like Richard Spencer. And just when the actors and musicians who popularized the undercut started to grow out their hair, Brolin's character has recently brought it back into the spotlight.

[Seriously Dude](https://matrix-skin.xianfengchem.com/blog/7011712357.html)

Now, if you're running out the door to your barber or reaching for the hair trimmers to achieve Brolin's extreme cut, not so fast.

"I think that it has, like the man-bun, run its course," says Boswell Scot, owner of Best Barber in New York City. "Guys are trending to more classic styles with two primary choices: super short, classic, think Michael Fassbender, or longer hair, think Orlando Bloom."

The undercut might look great in a movie that features a machine-gun-toting cyborg, but in real life, it's just going to look out of place. Scot says the haircut works for Brolin in the movie, but the style isn't unique or groundbreaking in the world of men's cuts.

This means fewer hairstyles like, say, David Beckham's, and more like Kit Harington's. (Just don't forget to condition your mane.) So if you want to get Brolin's look, the next time you head to the barber, maybe go ahead and skip his Cable cut and instead focus on those mechanical drop sets.
