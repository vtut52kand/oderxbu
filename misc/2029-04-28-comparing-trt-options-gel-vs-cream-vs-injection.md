TRT injections are most widely used as there’s more compliance and rapid absorption of treatment. Ultimately, there is no one-size-fits-all answer to which TRT method is best. Is testosterone gel better than injections?

TRT injections are better than TRT gels when it comes to factors like rapid absorption since injections are delivered directly into your bloodstream and offer maximum effect. Which is better, testosterone pills or injections?

Testosterone pills take longer to get absorbed compared to the injection. Whether to go for the pills or the injection depends on your preference. Consider the pros and cons of each and determine which best suits you.

Is testosterone gel the same as cream?

No, testosterone gel is not the same as cream. Testosterone gel typically has a gel-like consistency, while cream may have a thicker or creamier texture. The absorption rate may vary between gel and cream formulations, with gel generally being absorbed more quickly.
