Explanation

Prostate cancer in its final stages can cause a range of symptoms that affect a person's daily life.

Common Symptoms:

Pain: Many people experience significant pain, especially in the bones, as cancer can spread there.

Fatigue: Extreme tiredness is common and can make it hard to do everyday activities.

Weight Loss: Losing weight without trying can happen as the body uses more energy to fight the cancer.

Urinary Problems: Difficulty urinating, or needing to go urgently, can occur as the prostate enlarges.

These symptoms can vary, but they often become more severe as the cancer progresses.
