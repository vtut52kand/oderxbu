During quarantine, many celebrities have been making some major hair changes, such as Blake Shelton rocking some serious Tiger King hair and Chris Pratt going full-on Wolverine during a recent Instagram live. The latest star to switch up his look, though, is returning to his roots. Jackass's Johnny Knoxville debuted his gray hair on Instagram and revealed that it's actually his natural color.

In the new Instagram photo of himself, Knoxville revealed that his signature brunette hair color that his fans are accustomed to seeing is actually a dye job. "Don’t tell anyone but I’m no longer a natural brunette," he wrote in the caption to the post. "I haven’t been for over 20 years so today I decided to cut my hair down to the roots to match my quarantine beard."

Knoxville is just one of the latest celebrity men to decide to buzz their hair while in quarantine. With barbershops shut down, many people are turning to at-home haircuts, and a buzz down to the root a la Knoxville seems like the easiest thing to do. Actors Anthony Rapp and Riz Ahmed recently shaved their heads while Chris Evans took sheers to his brother's scalp. Athletes David Beckham and Cristiano Ronaldo are also getting in on the shorter looks.
