# Rock This Style Accessory, Support the Next Generation Of Rock Stars

Elvis and Michael Jackson preferred theirs black. The Rolling Stones liked theirs tight. The Ramones wore theirs until they had holes in them.

Levi’s have long been the uniform of rock stars through the generations, so it’s fitting that the iconic denim label is creating new music partnerships that give underprivileged kids a shot at music education.

And now you can help.

Buy a cool limited-edition Levi’s Music Project pin for your denim jacket online or in stores and a portion of the proceedings will be donated to the Levi’s Music Project.

The project’s most recent collaboration was just launched for the 2016–2017 school year with singer Alicia Keys. Together, the denim label and the R&B superstar introduced a new Music Technology program at Brooklyn’s Edward R. Murrow High School.

[Next Generation Of](https://glycometrix.snaketongueofficial.com/)

The long-term, community-based music initiative offers inner-city students access to sound engineering, audio-visual production and post-production, mastering, songwriting, and other creative opportunities. The kids will get the chance to expand their music education and study in a cutting-edge media lab equipped with the latest in sound production technology.

The goal of the program is to help artists give back to their communities and inspire the next generation of creative minds. It will eventually expand its reach to China, France, Germany, India and the U.K.

“Denim was the fabric woven into the counterculture of the 1960s,” said James Curleigh, Levi’s Executive Vice President and President of Global Brands, at the recent launch party for the Victoria & Albert Museum Exhibition, You Say You Want a Revolution? Records and Rebels 1966-70. The museum partnered with Levi’s for the exhibit. He noted the connection between the denim label and Woodstock, as well as the company’s continued passion for music.
