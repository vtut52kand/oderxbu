# 4 Guys Open Up About Staying Active With Psoriatic Arthritis

If you're one of the 1.5 million Americans living with psoriatic arthritis (PsA), you know all too well how emotionally and physically draining the condition can be. PsA has no cure. It impacts the skin and joints, causing symptoms like scaly skin patches and joint pain, the latter of which can be especially trying for those who enjoy working out. Thankfully, with the right treatment and lifestyle choices, it's possible to live a fulfilling, active life with PsA—and even control some of the most challenging symptoms.
