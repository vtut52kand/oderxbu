This is a great question, and we applaud your interest in finding the best products for your budget—instead of foregoing it all together. Just because a product is expensive doesn’t make it the best. You have plenty of options that won’t bust your wallet but still address your main concerns.

Drugstore brands like Aveeno and Nivea make more than just shave gel. You can find a product for every step of your grooming routine—including facial cleanser and aftershave lotion—at prices mostly under $10. While you’re walking down the aisles at the drugstore, you will also find an array of shampoos from Dove Men+Care and Head & Shoulders. From dandruff-fighting forumals to 3-in-1 washes, these brands get the job done just like those sold exclusively in upscale salons.

Let’s not forget Cetaphil, Neutrogena, and CeraVe. If you have sensitive skin, all three brands have products that will treat acne or moisturize without additives like color dyes and strong fragrances. Our favorites are Cetaphil Daily Facial Cleanse and Neutrogena Men Triple Protect Face Lotion SPF 20. And while you’re looking at Neutrogena, don’t forget to also protect your body—not just your face—from the sun’s UV rays.
