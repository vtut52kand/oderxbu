If your left arm is swollen and painful, consider these possible causes:

Injury or trauma: Sprains, strains, fractures, or overuse can cause swelling and pain. Infection: Skin infections like cellulitis can cause swelling, redness, warmth, and tenderness. Blood clots (deep vein thrombosis): A clot blocking blood flow can cause sudden swelling and pain. Lymphedema: Fluid buildup from lymphatic blockage may cause swelling and discomfort. Venous or arterial issues: Blockages or poor circulation can lead to swelling and pain. Other causes: Allergic reactions, inflammatory conditions, or nerve problems may also contribute.

Seek medical attention promptly if swelling and pain come on suddenly, worsen, or are accompanied by fever, chest pain, shortness of breath, or numbness.
