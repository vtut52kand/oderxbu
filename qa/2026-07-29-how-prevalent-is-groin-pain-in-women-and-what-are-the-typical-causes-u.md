Explanation

Groin pain in women can be a significant issue, particularly for those involved in sports. Studies show that female athletes often experience groin pain due to sports-related injuries. These injuries can include muscle strains or overuse injuries from repetitive movements.

In addition to sports injuries, hip problems can also lead to groin pain. This is often seen in team-sport athletes who engage in activities requiring quick direction changes or high-impact movements.

For older women, groin pain might be related to hernias, which can be diagnosed using specific imaging techniques.

Overall, identifying the exact cause of groin pain is crucial for effective treatment and recovery, whether it involves rest, physical therapy, or other medical interventions.
