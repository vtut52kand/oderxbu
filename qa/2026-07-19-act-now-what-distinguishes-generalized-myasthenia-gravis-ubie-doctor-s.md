Generalized myasthenia gravis (gMG) is an autoimmune neuromuscular disorder that causes widespread muscle weakness across multiple muscle groups throughout the body. Unlike ocular myasthenia gravis, which is limited to the eye muscles, gMG can affect the face, throat, limbs, and respiratory muscles, often worsening with activity and improving with rest.

Because symptoms like fatigue, drooping eyelids, difficulty swallowing, or limb weakness can overlap with many other conditions, identifying the cause early is critical to getting the right care.
