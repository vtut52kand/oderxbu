The pandemic has highlighted the risk of exposure to contagious illness when visiting a doctor’s office, leading many to seek in-home healthcare. If you’re wondering whether this kind of care is right for you, we look at what types of VIP medical services you can get at home.

What are VIP medical services?

Also known as concierge medicine, these providers typically come to your home for your appointments. Many concierge doctors also provide telemedicine options so that patients can get ongoing care remotely.

VIP medical services also require upfront payment for treatments. Doctors avoid the administrative headaches of dealing with insurance companies by offering two payment options: “a la carte”, meaning on a one-time/case by case basis, or through membership plans paid monthly.
