Erythritol is a sugar alcohol widely recognized as safe by the FDA and well-tolerated by most people. However, in rare cases, individuals may experience allergic reactions, including hives, itching, swelling, or digestive discomfort after consuming it. If you suspect an erythritol allergy or notice symptoms after eating foods containing this sweetener, it's wise to avoid it and consult a healthcare provider.
