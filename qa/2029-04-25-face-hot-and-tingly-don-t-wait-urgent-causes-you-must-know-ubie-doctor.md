What conditions might make my face feel hot and tingly?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

A hot and tingly sensation on the face is often caused by abnormal sweating patterns, exposure to certain chemicals, or nerve-related conditions such as neuropathy or nerve irritation. Other common triggers include anxiety, hormonal shifts, allergic reactions, rosacea, or circulation changes. While these sensations are frequently harmless and temporary, persistent or worsening symptoms may point to an underlying condition that needs attention.

[Causes You](https://reboundworks.escortskhi.com/)

Sometimes, your face might feel hot and tingly due to different conditions. One possibility is abnormal sweating patterns, which can also cause itching. This means your body might sweat in unusual ways, leading to these sensations.

Another reason could be exposure to certain chemicals. These can cause a tingling feeling known as paresthesia, which happens when nerves react to these chemicals.

Additionally, scalp dysesthesia is a condition where the scalp feels uncomfortable due to nerve issues. This can make the scalp feel hot or tingly.
