What is Post-Treatment Lyme Disease Syndrome (PTLDS)?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Post-Treatment Lyme Disease Syndrome (PTLDS) is a condition in which symptoms such as fatigue, pain, and cognitive difficulties persist for six months or longer after completing antibiotic treatment for Lyme disease, even though the original infection has been cleared.
