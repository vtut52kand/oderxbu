What is Scalp Dysesthesia?

Scalp Dysesthesia: This is a condition where people feel discomfort or a burning feeling on their scalp or face, even though there is nothing visible on the skin.

Neuropathic Phenomenon: This means that the feeling is caused by problems with the nerves, not by something you can see, like a rash or a burn.
