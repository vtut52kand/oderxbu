Thanks for writing. It’s important to tackle the bumps as soon as they appear to prevent them from forming into lifelong scars.

“Razor bumps are caused by ingrown hairs that get trapped inside the pore,” says Marina Peredo, M.D., F.A.A.D, founder of Spatique Medical Spa in Smithtown, NY. Back off a little bit, she advises, and instead of aiming for a super-close shave, leave the hair a little shorter to minimize the likelihood of bumps forming. Also, take notice of how you’re shaving. “Always shave in the downward direction using a clean single or double-bladed razor,” she says. Any more than that will add unnecessary aggression to the troubled area. And don’t be afraid to take a break from shaving your head—a razor over the already bumpy surface will only irritate your skin and cause more of the unsightly bumps.

Or, visit a doctor who can prescribe a heavy-duty treatment. A topical medication containing hydrocortisone, retinoids, or antibiotics will effectively treat the area, Dr. Peredo says.
