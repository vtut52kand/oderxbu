Hemorrhoids are caused by veins around the rectum and anus that swell and enlarge with blood leading to symptoms like itching, pain and bleeding. There are two types of hemorrhoids: internal and external. Internal hemorrhoids are generally painless and cannot be felt externally unless they have prolapsed (ie: pushed out through the anus). External hemorrhoids, on the other hand, can be painful, itchy and also bleed. These can be more easily felt and seen around the anus.
