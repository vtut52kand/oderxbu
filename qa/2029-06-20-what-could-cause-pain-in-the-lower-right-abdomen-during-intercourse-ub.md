What could cause pain in the lower right abdomen during intercourse?

Reviewed by Tomas Diaz, MD

[lower right abdomen](https://tiancansz.com/tj97a6c/)

Emergency Medicine

Pain in the lower right abdomen during intercourse can sometimes be caused by conditions like endometriosis or pelvic floor dysfunction.

Important Next Steps

Not seeing your question? No worries.

Explanation

When you feel pain in your lower right abdomen during or after sex, one common reason is endometriosis. This condition happens when tissue similar to the lining of your uterus grows outside of it, causing pain that may be more noticeable during certain times or activities. Another possible cause is ovarian cysts, which are fluid-filled sacs that can cause discomfort if they become large or twist. Issues with the muscles of the pelvic floor (the muscles which hold the pelvic organs including the uterus in place), can contribute to pain after sex. Because there are several different causes, it is a good idea to share your symptoms with a healthcare provider. They can help figure out what might be causing the pain and identify the best treatment.
