What are the common causes of excessive daytime sleepiness?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Excessive daytime sleepiness (EDS) is most commonly caused by sleep disorders such as sleep apnea, insomnia, and narcolepsy. Other frequent causes include insufficient sleep, poor sleep quality, certain medications, and underlying medical conditions like depression, anemia, or thyroid problems.

Because EDS can stem from many different conditions—some easily managed, others requiring medical treatment—identifying the root cause is essential. Taking a free, instant, online symptom check can help you understand what may be driving your fatigue and guide you toward the right next steps.
