How to Use Delay Sprays Correctly

Follow these steps for best results:

Wash and dry the penis.

Apply 1–3 sprays to the head and underside.

Gently rub it in for even coverage.

Wait 5–15 minutes for absorption.

Wipe off excess before intercourse (if instructed).

Important tips:

Start with the lowest dose.

Do not exceed recommended amounts.

Avoid broken or irritated skin.

Wash hands after application.

How to Use Delay Wipes Correctly

Open the packet.

Wipe evenly over the head of the penis.

Allow to air dry for 5–10 minutes.

Do not rinse unless directed.

Tips:

Use only one wipe unless directed otherwise.

Allow full drying time to prevent partner numbness.

Dispose properly after use.

Side Effects to Know About

Most men tolerate these products well. However, possible side effects include:

Mild skin irritation

Burning sensation

Reduced erection firmness (if overused)

Rarely:

Allergic reaction

Severe irritation

If you notice persistent redness, swelling, rash, or pain, stop use and speak to a doctor.
