Explanation

Sensitive skin is a condition that can make your face feel like it is burning. Some studies suggest it can be considered a neuropathic disorder, which means it is related to the nerves in your skin. People with sensitive skin often feel burning, stinging, or itching when their skin comes into contact with certain products or environmental factors.

Key Points:

Sensitive Skin: This condition can make your skin react strongly to things like soap, lotion, or even weather changes. This can cause a burning feeling.

Why It Happens: The nerves in the skin become more active and react to things that usually wouldn't bother other people.

How It Feels: Besides burning, you might feel itching or stinging.
