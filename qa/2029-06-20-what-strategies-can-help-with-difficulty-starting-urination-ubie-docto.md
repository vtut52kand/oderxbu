Possible Causes

Benign Prostatic Hyperplasia (BPH): This condition is common in older men and can lead to urinary problems. Managing BPH can help improve urination.

Medications: Some drugs might cause urinary retention, making it hard to start urinating. Identifying and adjusting these medications can be beneficial.
