About the Symptom

Hard, thick areas of skin are usually due to callus formation, but can also be caused by certain infections, certain skin cancers, and genetic skin conditions.

Possible Causes

Generally, Rough, hard skin can be related to:

Psoriatic Arthritis is an inflammatory joint condition associated with the skin disorder Psoriasis, which causes red, scaly patches on the skin. Joint pain, swelling and stiffness are typical signs of Psoriatic Arthritis.

A skin disease caused by the immune system attacking the skin cells, which leads to red-brown reas of thickened skin with a silvery scale. It is thought to have some genetic predisposition and then triggered by factors such as environment, infections, and stress. Rarely it can be due to medications.

A highly contagious disease caused by Scabies mites. It can be transmitted through skin-to-skin contact or contact with the clothes or furniture of infected individuals.
