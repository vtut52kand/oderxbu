Wegovy Side Effects: What to Know

Wegovy commonly causes digestive side effects, including:

Nausea and vomiting

Diarrhea or constipation

Bloating and abdominal pain

Headache and fatigue

These occur because Wegovy slows stomach emptying. Symptoms typically ease over time and can be managed with slower dose escalation, smaller low-fat meals, and steady hydration.

Severe or persistent abdominal pain

Repeated vomiting or dehydration

Allergic reactions or vision changes

Signs of gallbladder issues or pancreatitis

Wegovy Side Effects: Why Your Body Is Struggling & Medical Next Steps

If you've started Wegovy (semaglutide) and you're not feeling quite like yourself, you're not alone. Wegovy side effects are common, especially in the first few weeks of treatment. For many people, symptoms are mild and improve over time. For others, they can feel disruptive or concerning.

Understanding why these side effects happen — and what to do next — can help you make informed, confident decisions about your health.

What Is Wegovy and How Does It Work?

Wegovy is a prescription medication approved for chronic weight management in adults and certain adolescents with obesity or overweight with related health conditions.

It contains semaglutide, a medication that mimics a natural hormone in your body called GLP-1 (glucagon-like peptide-1). This hormone:

Slows how quickly your stomach empties

Reduces appetite

Increases feelings of fullness

Helps regulate blood sugar

These effects can lead to significant weight loss. However, because Wegovy changes how your digestive system and brain communicate, side effects are most common in the gastrointestinal (GI) system.

The majority of Wegovy side effects are digestive and tend to occur when starting the medication or increasing the dose.

Why These Happen

Wegovy slows stomach emptying. Food stays in your stomach longer, which can:

Make you feel full quickly

Cause nausea if you eat too much

Lead to bloating or reflux

Trigger constipation in some people

This is not your body "rejecting" the medication — it's your digestive system adapting to a new rhythm.

Less Common but More Serious Wegovy Side Effects

While rare, some side effects require prompt medical attention.

Call a Doctor Right Away If You Experience:

Severe abdominal pain that doesn't go away (possible pancreatitis)

Pain in the upper right abdomen with fever or yellowing of the skin (possible gallbladder problems)

Persistent vomiting leading to dehydration

Signs of allergic reaction (rash, swelling, difficulty breathing)

Vision changes (especially in people with diabetes)

There is also a boxed warning about the potential risk of thyroid C-cell tumors seen in animal studies. While this has not been confirmed in humans, Wegovy is not recommended for people with:

Personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma

Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia syndrome type 2 (MEN 2)

If you notice a lump in your neck, trouble swallowing, or persistent hoarseness, speak to a doctor promptly.

Not everyone reacts the same way to Wegovy. If your side effects feel intense, here are possible reasons:

1. Dose Escalation Is Too Fast

Wegovy is designed to be increased gradually. If doses are raised too quickly, nausea and vomiting are more likely.

2. Eating Habits Haven't Adjusted

Because stomach emptying slows, large meals can trigger symptoms. Fatty or fried foods often worsen nausea.

3. Dehydration

Vomiting or diarrhea can lead to dehydration, which worsens fatigue, dizziness, and headaches.

4. Underlying Gallbladder Issues

Rapid weight loss itself increases the risk of gallstones, which can cause significant pain.

5. Pre-Existing GI Sensitivity

If you have a history of reflux, IBS, or other digestive disorders, symptoms may feel amplified.

You should not stop Wegovy without speaking to your doctor. However, urgent evaluation is needed if you develop:

Long-Term Safety Considerations

Clinical trials have shown that many people tolerate Wegovy well over time. However:

Ongoing monitoring is important

Routine follow-up visits are necessary

Blood tests may be recommended

Wegovy works best as part of a comprehensive plan that includes:

Nutrition changes

Physical activity

Behavioral support

Medical supervision

It is not meant to replace healthy habits — it is meant to support them.

Should You Be Worried?

It's normal to feel concerned when your body reacts to a new medication. The key is to distinguish between:

Expected adjustment symptoms (common and often temporary)

Serious warning signs (rare but urgent)

Most Wegovy side effects are manageable and improve with time, dose adjustments, and dietary changes.

However, persistent or severe symptoms should never be ignored.
