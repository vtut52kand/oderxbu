About the Symptom

The feeling like something is stuck in your throat is also known as a globus sensation. It is a symptom that can make one feel like having a lump in the throat. It can be due to any number of conditions including reflux (heartburn), post-nasal drip, food or medicine getting stuck in the throat, and, less commonly, masses in the throat.
