What can I do to prevent lower back pain when sneezing?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Lower back pain when sneezing is often triggered by the sudden pressure a sneeze places on your spine, discs, and surrounding muscles. To help prevent it, maintain good posture throughout the day, engage your core, and brace your back by placing a hand on a stable surface before you sneeze. Strengthening your core and staying flexible can also reduce your risk.

That said, recurring back pain during sneezing may signal an underlying issue like a herniated disc, muscle strain, or nerve irritation—conditions that benefit from early identification. Tips to Help Prevent Pain:

Good Posture: Stand or sit up straight when you feel a sneeze coming. This can help reduce the strain on your back muscles.
