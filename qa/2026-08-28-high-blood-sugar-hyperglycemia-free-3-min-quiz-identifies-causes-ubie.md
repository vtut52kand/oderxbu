About the Symptom

High blood sugar (hyperglycemia) occurs when blood glucose levels are above normal range, typically above 180-200 mg/dL. It's commonly associated with diabetes and can lead to serious complications if left untreated.

When to See a Doctor

Seek professional care if you experience any of the following symptoms

Extreme thirst

Frequent urination

Blurred vision

Fatigue and weakness

Confusion or difficulty concentrating

Fruity-smelling breath
