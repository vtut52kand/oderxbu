How many falls are considered too many and warrant medical attention?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

If someone falls often, it might be a sign that they need medical help. It's important to check with a doctor if falls happen more than once or twice, especially in older adults.

Falls can be serious, especially for older adults. If someone falls more than once or twice, it might be time to see a doctor. According to the provided references, frequent falls can be a warning sign of other health problems.

Frequent Falls: The first reference notes that older adults who call 911 often for falls might have underlying health issues. This suggests that even one fall could warrant attention if it leads to emergency calls.

Prevention: The second reference discusses how prevention is important. If someone falls, doctors can help find out why and suggest ways to stop more falls from happening.

Risk Factors: The third reference talks about things that make falls more likely, like balance problems or weak muscles. Understanding these can help in preventing falls.

In summary, if falls are happening often, it's important to get medical advice. This can help find out why falls are happening and how to prevent them in the future.
