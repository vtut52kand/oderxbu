Why is there a green spot on my nail after removing acrylics?

Reviewed by Garrett Kneese, MD, MPH

Family Medicine

A green spot on your nail after removing acrylics might be due to a condition called Green Nail Syndrome, often caused by bacteria.

When you see a green spot on your nail after taking off acrylics, it could be due to a condition known as Green Nail Syndrome. This is often caused by a type of bacteria that likes to grow in damp places. When you have acrylic nails, moisture can get trapped between the acrylic and your natural nail, creating a perfect environment for these bacteria to grow.

What Happens:

Trapped Moisture: Acrylic nails can trap moisture between the acrylic and your natural nail.

[on my nail after](https://musclemend.sunsimiaosd.com/blog/5957635770.html)

Bacterial Growth: This moisture allows bacteria to grow, sometimes leading to a greenish color on the nail.

What You Can Do:

Keep Nails Dry: Make sure your nails are dry and clean to prevent bacteria from growing.

See a Professional: If the green spot doesn’t go away, you might want to see a healthcare professional for advice.

Remember, while acrylic nails can be fun, it's important to take care of your natural nails too!
