What Does a Headache in the Back of the Head Indicate?

The most common cause for headaches in the back of your head is tension, which has led this type of pain to be known as tension headaches. The tension can stem from stress at home or the workplace, poor sitting and standing posture, or numerous other things. Unfortunately, there isn’t any single cause for tension headaches, and they seem to affect people in a variety of ways.

Pain All Over the Back of the Head

When the pain is widespread throughout the back of your head, it’s almost always a tension headache and isn’t cause for alarm. It will feel like you’re wearing a headband or baseball cap that’s too small. The only time you should worry about pain throughout the back of your head is when they’re secondary headaches caused by an underlying condition.

The only way to be sure that an underlying condition doesn’t cause your headache is to schedule a visit with a doctor, explain your symptoms, and allow them to examine you.

Headaches in the back of the head with neck pain

Headaches in the back of the head accompanied by neck pain can indicate arthritis, low spinal fluid pressure on the brain, or a herniated disc, all of which require a medical diagnosis. They can also be caused by referred pain from a tension headache, but neck pain often indicates something more.
