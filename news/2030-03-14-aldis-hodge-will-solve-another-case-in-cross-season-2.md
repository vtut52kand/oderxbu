THROUGH THE YEARS Prime Video has produced a lot of television throughout a lot of genres and a lot of it has been good. But one place where the streamer has particularly thrived is in the world of Dad Lit adaptations. Between Jack Ryan (based on Tom Clancy's character of the same name), Reacher (based on Lee Child's hit series), and, now, Cross (based on James Patterson's long-running detective), Prime Video has found its niche and is only getting better and better at it.

Cross, the latest entry in this subgenre, is a well-crafted piece of television that sets up a creepy mystery and allows a talented, (mostly) well-adjusted detective to figure it out and take out the bad guy. In a way, that's comforting—Aldis Hodge plays Alex Cross as a cop who understands his responsibility to society while also managing and dealing with his own demons.

What makes Cross unique in the cop show landscape is how the lead characters, our heroes, feel no real loyalty to "the system." As Hodge said in a recent Men's Health profile, he and his partner John Sampson (Isaiah Mustafa) just want to take down the bad guys, no matter who they are. "It could be a suspect, a serial killer, or it could be somebody else with a badge," he said. "It doesn’t matter. The thing that makes these guys special is they’re going to do the job the right way, regardless of the risks."

Cross solved the mystery in his first go-around on Prime Video. But will there be more? Luckily, we know the answer to that: it's a definitive yes.
