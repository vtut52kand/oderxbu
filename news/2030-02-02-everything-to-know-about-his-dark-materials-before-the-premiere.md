# Everything to Know About 'His Dark Materials' Before the Premiere

While fans may still be mourning the end of Game of Thrones, HBO is partnering with BBC to bring viewers a show just like it: His Dark Materials. The good news: It's coming this Monday. The better news: The series is based on the trilogy of fantasy novels by Philip Pullman, and the books are all completed, meaning we're likely to see a true adaptation—not the meandering narrative-guessing that plagued the other fantasy show. No, His Dark Materials will adapt the beloved, completed source material for TV—the first book in the series, The Golden Compass, was adapted into a movie in 2007—and feature a prestige cast, including James McAvoy, Lin-Manuel Miranda, and Dafne Keen, who you might remember as the little girl kicking some serious ass in Logan.

Though broadcasted stateside by HBO, His Dark Materials was primarily produced by the BBC, which reportedly spent an enormous sum of money on the series' special effects, making it the network's most expensive series to date. Given the importance of animals and creatures to the story (all of which needed to be animated), the expenses seem justified. So if it's at all like Game of Thrones, it would be the equivalent of animating Dire wolves and dragons for literally every single episode. And we all know what happens when you skimp on the Dire wolf budget: you get your hero walking away from his pet without a goodbye pat.

Which is all to say, we're excited for His Dark Materials and the many CGI animals to come.

Who is in the His Dark Materials cast?

The show will star James McAvoy (Glass) as Lord Asriel, Ruth Wilson (Luther) as Mrs Coulter, and Dafne Keen (Logan) as the young protagonist, Lyra. The series will also feature James Cosmo (Game of Thrones, Chernobyl) as Fader Corum, leader of the nomadic gyptian tribe who take Lyra under their wing, Clarke Peters (The Wire) as Dr. Carne, and Andrew Scott (or just the "Hot Priest" to Fleabag fans) as Colonel John Parry. The character of Lee Scoresby, an adventurous airship pilot, will be played by none other than Lin-Manuel Miranda.

What's His Dark Materials about?

Based on the best-selling books, His Dark Materials is a fantasy adventure that follows Lyra, an orphan growing up in Jordan College, Oxford, as she stumbles upon a conspiracy involving her uncle, Lord Asriel, the mysterious Mrs Coulter, and a growing number of missing children.

The series is set in an alternate reality where every human being has an animal companion called a daemon. Somewhere between a patronus and a spirit animal, daemons are manifestations of the human soul and share personality traits with their human.
