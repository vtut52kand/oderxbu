IF FALLOUT SEASON 1 was simply introducing viewers to its world—more than 200 years after a nuclear war transformed/destroyed a retro-futuristic world into a radioactive wasteland—then season 2 was pulling the microscope back, somehow managing to make that world even bigger.

Throughout the course of the show we not only got to know our heroes (played by Ella Purnell, Walton Goggins, and Aaron Moten) and even our villains (played by Kyle MacLachlan and Justin Theroux) better, but we got to see the internal machinations that both made this disaster possible, and how the destructive nature of humanity persists long after "the world ends." Through the adventures of Lucy (Purnell) and The Ghoul (Goggins), we learn of warring factions like NCR and The Legion; Through Maximus's (Moten) story, we learn of the potentially tyrannical overreach of The Brotherhood of Steel. It all comes together to make for an interesting slow burn that not only advances our overall story and tells us more about who all these characters are, but also helps to make the Fallout world feel huge and lived-in.

Who will be in the Fallout season 3 cast?

Walton Goggins, Ella Purnell, and Aaron Moten are the core lead cast members of Fallout, and we will once again see them in season 3. Characters like Thaddeus (Johnny Pemberton), Norm (Moises Arias), Steph (Annabel O'Hagan), and Barb (Frances Turner) have also become staples, and feel like certainties to return. Ron Perlman was also introduced as a super mutant in season 2, and while that could just be a fun inside-baseball cameo (he's a longtime narrator of the video games), it also feels likely that we'll see way more of his character going forward.

Part of the genius of Fallout taking place both in the wasteland, but also in pre-blast flashbacks, is that it allows the show to keep characters around constantly (think about how we saw Lee Moldaver in season 2, despite her death in season 1). Through this principle, it feels likely that villains like Hank (Kyle MacLachlan) and Mr. House (Justin Theroux) will also be able to remain part of the show for the entirety of its run, one way or another. After all, a good villain goes a long way!

When will Fallout season 3 come out on Prime Video?

That we don't know. But the wait between season 1 and season 2 was about a year and a half, and for a show as ambitious as Fallout—with major production value and in-demand actors—that feels more than reasonable. We'll keep our eyes peeled for an early Summer 2027 release.
