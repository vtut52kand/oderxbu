Age-related macular degeneration (AMD) is an eye condition that affects central vision and comes in two main forms: dry AMD and wet AMD.

Dry AMD: The more common form, accounting for about 80-90% of cases. It progresses slowly and is caused by the gradual thinning of the macula.

Wet AMD: Less common but more serious, this form can cause rapid and severe vision loss due to abnormal blood vessel growth under the retina.

Early detection is critical to protecting your vision. Because AMD symptoms—like blurred central vision, distorted lines, or difficulty recognizing faces—can overlap with other eye conditions, understanding what you're experiencing is an important first step.
